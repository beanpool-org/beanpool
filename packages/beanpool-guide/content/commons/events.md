## Finding events

- In the **Market**, tap **Events**. Choose **Today**, **This weekend** or **Next 7 days**.
- On the **Map**, events are purple pins. On the phone, tap **Events** to see only them. In the web app they are always there, and the date chips narrow them.

## Your events

In the **Market**, tap **★ For You**. **Your events** is the first thing on it: everything you have said you are **Going** to or **Interested** in, soonest first. Tap one to open it.

It is only there when you have something coming up, and it holds only your own answers — nobody else can see it.

## Going

On an event, tap **Going** or **Interested**. Tap your choice again to clear it.

When you are **Going**:

- you can read the host's **Note for people who are going**, once the host lets you in, where the host chose that;
- you can open the **event chat**. See "Event chat";
- for an online event, you see its **Link to join** and a **Join** button, on the same terms as the note.

Tap the event to see everything, and **Show on map**. On the phone it opens your maps app. In the web app it shows the place on the Map.

## Being reminded

When you are **Going** or **Interested**, the event shows **Remind me** and how long before it starts you will be told — **1 day before (your default)** until you change it.

- Tap **Change** to pick **1 week before**, **1 day before**, **2 hours before**, **1 hour before** or **30 minutes before**. You can tick more than one.
- Tick nothing to get no reminder for this event.
- Then tap **Save for this event**. A tick is not saved until you do.
- Tap **Use my default** to hand it back to your usual choice.

To change your usual choice for every event, see "Notifications".

Reminders come as alerts on your phone, with your Marketplace alerts, so they stop if you switch those off. You are never reminded about an event that was cancelled or has ended, and never about a time that had already passed when you said you were coming.

## Telling someone about it

Open the event and tap **Share**. Your phone's usual share sheet opens with the event's name, when it is, where it is, and a link. On a computer, the link is copied for you to paste.

Anyone who taps that link opens the event — in the app if they have it, in the web app otherwise. Someone who is not in your community lands on its join page. In a web browser, the global community shows them the event first, as a visitor sees it, and opens it again once they have joined.

## Putting it in your calendar

Open the event and tap **Add to calendar**.

- On the **web app**, a calendar file downloads. Open it and your calendar offers to add the event.
- On an **iPhone**, the same file is handed to your phone, which offers **Add to Calendar**.
- On **Android**, Google Calendar opens with the event already filled in. Check it and save.

The entry carries the name, the time, the place and the link back to the event. If the host later changes the time, your calendar does not change with it — the app will still remind you, and the event itself always shows the new time.

## Creating an event

- On **Commons**, tap **Start something** (on the phone, the round **+** button) and pick **Event**. Or tap **+ ADD POST** on the Market tab or the Map, then **Event**.
- **Title**, and when it **Starts**. **Ends** is optional; it is 2 hours later unless you change it.
- Times are in the event's own time zone, taken from the pin. When that is not your own zone, a line under the times names it. Tap **Change** beside it if it is wrong.
- **Repeats:** choose how often it comes round, such as every week on that day or once a month, or leave it at **Does not repeat**. **Until** is optional: leave it blank and the event repeats until you cancel it.
- Choose **At a place** or **Online**. For an online event there is no place and no pin: add a **Link to join** instead. Only you and the people going see it (where you let people in, only the people you let in). Its times are in your own time zone, and everyone else sees them in theirs, marked **your time**.
- **Place name**, like "Town hall".
- Put the pin on the map: tap the map, search **Find an address**, or tap **Use my location**. Tap **Approximate (~100 m)** if you do not want the exact spot shown. See "Where your pin goes".
- A **Description**, and at least one **Photo** (every new event needs a photo).
- **Note for people who are going:** everyone who taps Going sees it, unless you tick **Only people I let in see the note and chat** (ticked to start with on the global community, where anyone can join in a minute): then only the people you let in see it, and the event chat. A good place for the exact address or a gate code; the place name is for everyone.
- **Who can see it:** your whole community, or one group.
- If you keep a project or enterprise or are a convenor of a group, **Post as** lets you post the event in its name. A group hosts for its own members only; an enterprise hosts for the whole community, so an enterprise event is never group-only.
- Tap **Create**. Your event is in the Market and on the Map.

In the web app the title box is **What's happening**, the pin buttons are **📍 My location** and **📌 Drop a pin**, and the last button is **Create Event**.

You can have 5 upcoming events at a time. A group's events count against the group, and an enterprise's against the enterprise.

## Running your event

Open your event. As the host you can:

- see **Who's going**, and let people in: **Let in** beside a name (tap again to take it back), or **Let everyone in**. Your own friends are let in as they tap Going. If you untick **Only people I let in see the note and chat**, everyone going sees the note and the chat so far, and the chat says the host opened it;
- **Edit event**. If you change the time or the place, everyone going is told;
- **Copy to a new date**, to run it again;
- **Cancel event**, then confirm (**Cancel event** again on the phone, **OK** in the web app). Everyone going can see it is cancelled, and its chat becomes read-only.

## Repeating events

A repeating event shows **↻** and how often beside its date, such as **Every Friday** or **Monthly on the 16th**. Its card shows the next date; open it to see **Next dates**.

If you host it, each of the next dates has:

- **Cancel this date:** that day only is cancelled. Everyone going that day is told.
- **Change this date only:** that day is cancelled and you make a one-off on another date.
- **Bring it back:** a cancelled date is on again.

**Cancel event** cancels every date, and its chat becomes read-only. Until then the chat stays open between dates, up to the last date; with no **Until**, it stays open until you cancel the event.
