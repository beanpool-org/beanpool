**How this community works** is the owner's screen for the structural settings that define what your community does.

Only an owner can change these settings. Admins can view every setting and its reasons, but cannot make changes. When an owner changes a member-facing setting, every member is automatically notified on Home with the change and any optional note the owner includes.

## Who can join

- **Who can join**: Choose whether any member can invite new people, only admins can invite, or new members are paused for now. On a global server, the door is open to anyone.
- **Requests to join**: Allow people who find your community on the global node to ask to join. A member or admin answers with an invite.
- **Members may share listings outside the community**: On by default. A member can share one listing or event with someone outside BeanPool: the link shows that one post for 7 days, under the rules a visitor gets (what it is, its photos, its price and its area; never a name, a face or the exact place), and leads to your community's own card and Ask to join. Members stop their own links under **Settings**, **Shared outside**. Turned off, every link already shared stops at once and stays stopped if you turn it on again. Always off during a private preview. Your server keeps only a fingerprint of each link, never the link itself.
- **Private preview**: Set on the server (PRIVATE_PREVIEW). While on, only people invited by an owner or admin can enter and visitors see nothing.

## Money

- **Beans**: The medium of trade, credit and the Commons. Once any member has traded and the ledger has moved, Beans are locked on and cannot be turned off.
- **Known floor for confirmed members**: How far confirmed members on the names list may go into debt while keeping an offer listed. Requires Beans to be on.

## Talking

- **First message from someone new**: When on, a message from someone new waits under Requests until accepted. When off, messages arrive straight away.

## Deciding together

- **Community votes (Decisions)**: Turn formal community proposals and votes on or off.

## Being found

- **Listed in the BeanPool directory**: Whether this community is published in the directory and map at beanpool.org.
- **Share location & radius**: Whether non-members can see your community's rough location on the map.
- **Share member count, email, phone, health**: Choose which details are sent to the public directory.
- **Visitors**: What non-members see when visiting this server.

## Safety

- **Limits on new accounts**: Small daily posting and messaging limits for new members' first days.
- **Reported posts hidden automatically**: Automatically hide posts reported by three independent groups until a moderator reviews them.
- **Repeat offenders muted automatically**: Automatically mute members with multiple takedowns.

## Connections

- **Connections to other communities**: Whether this node connects to peer communities (ENABLE_PEER_CONNECTORS). Set on the server.

## Changes

The audit log at the bottom lists every structural change made to the community, including who changed it, when, what changed, and any note to members.
