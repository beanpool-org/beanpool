## When to use it

A post has one price for one deal. For a batch of the same thing, such as 20 solar panels, jars of honey or tomato seedlings, price the Offer **Each**. It holds three things:

- the **price for one**,
- **how many you have**, and
- if you want, a **price for all of them**.

The examples on this page are a seller, Rob, with 20 solar panels at 300 Beans each, or all 20 for 5,500 Beans.

A buyer then chooses how many to take up. A post with one price works exactly as before. **Each** is for Offers only: a Need, a thing you lend, and a weekly or monthly post cannot be priced **Each**.

## Posting it

Start a post as usual (see "Posting an Offer or a Need"), choose **Offer**, and choose **Each** as the unit, right after **Total**. Three boxes appear:

- **PRICE FOR ONE:** what one costs. Tap **FREE** (in the web app, type 0) to give them away.
- **HOW MANY DO YOU HAVE?** A whole number from 1 to 9,999.
- **OR ALL OF THEM FOR (OPTIONAL):** one price for every item together. Leave it empty to sell them one by one only.

A line under the last box tells you where you stand:

- Empty: **Leave this empty to sell them one by one only.**
- A good price: **All 20 for 5,500 Beans. One by one they come to 6,000.**
- Not less than buying them one by one: **One by one, 20 come to 6,000 Beans. A price for all of them has to be less.** The post is refused until you lower it.

Under the price, the app shows what you receive after the 1.5% market fee, for one and for all: **1.5% goes to the community. You receive 295.50 Beans for each one, or 5,417.50 Beans for all of them.** A free post has no price for all of them, and a price for all of them is for 2 or more items.

If you had already typed the whole price as the **Total** and then choose **Each**, that figure moves to the box for all of them, and the box for one is left empty for you to fill in.

The rest of the form is as for any Offer: at least one photo, tags, a place and who can see it. **Repeatable** is not offered on an Offer priced **Each**: the count takes its place, and the post closes when everything is sold.

## What buyers see

On a card, the line reads **300 Beans each · 20 left · or all for 5,500**. On a small card, the price sits at the end of the row as **300 each**, with **20 left · or all for 5,500** under the title, and a tile shows **20 LEFT**. A free one reads **Free** with **20 left** under it. On the post itself, the price box shows the price for one, how many are left, and **or all 20 for 5,500 Beans**.

The price for all of them is for exactly that many. It shows only while every one is still there:

- The moment someone takes one on its own, it stops showing, and the post reads **19 left**. A price for all 20 does not fit 19.
- If that deal is cancelled, the 20 are back and the price for all of them shows again.
- If some are sold for good, open **Edit Post** and set a new price for what is left.

When none are left but a deal is still under way, the post says **All taken for now**. If that deal is cancelled, they come back. When none are left and every deal is finished, the post closes as **Sold out**, like any Offer that sells.

## Taking some up

Open the Offer and tap **🤝 Accept Offer**. A box asks **How many would you like?**

- **Choose how many:** use **−** and **+**, or type the number. It is a whole number from 1 to what is left, and the box shows the price for one and how many are left.
- **All 20 for 5,500 Beans:** shown only while the price for all of them is on offer, with how much less it is than one by one. Stepping up to all 20 picks this choice for you, so nobody pays 6,000 where 5,500 is offered.

Under the choices, the box shows what you will pay and what happens to it: **3 × 300 = 900 Beans**, then **900 Beans go into trust now. Rob receives 886.50 when you release them (1.5% goes to the community).** For all of them: **All 20 for 5,500 Beans**, and **5,500 Beans go into trust now. Rob receives 5,417.50 when you release them (1.5% goes to the community).**

Tap **Confirm**. Exactly the Beans shown are held in trust, and in the same step those items leave the count, so nobody else can take them. There is no waiting for the seller to say yes, and no way to ask first and agree later. As with any Offer, you can have one deal at a time on the same post.

If something changed while you were looking, the box says so and shows the post as it is now. Nothing is ever held at an amount you did not see. You may read:

- **Only 2 left now. Choose 2 or fewer.**
- **They have all been taken up.**
- **One has just been taken, so the price for all of them is off. You can still take what is left one by one.**
- **The price changed while you were looking. Check it and try again.**
- **Choose a whole number, 1 or more.**

## The deal, on both sides

The buyer's post reads **✅ You took up 3 on 11 Nov · 900 Beans held in trust** (for all of them, **✅ You took up all 20 on 11 Nov · 5,500 Beans held in trust**). The seller is told: **Dana took 3 of "Solar Panels". 900 Beans are held in trust.**

The seller's post reads **17 left · 3 held for buyers**, and under **Deals under way** each deal is a row: **Dana · 3 for 900 Beans held · since 11 Nov**, or **Dana · all 20 for 5,500 Beans held · since 11 Nov**. In **Your deals** and in the chat, the deal's title is **3 × Solar Panels**, or **All 20 × Solar Panels**.

The rest is as in "A deal, step by step". Do the job or hand over the thing, and the buyer taps **Release Beans** when it is done. A deal for a number of items pays what was held: there is no box for how many you got. If fewer changed hands, either of you cancels the deal and the buyer takes up the right number again. The 1.5% market fee is taken once, from the whole deal, and goes to your community's Commons.

## How the count moves

The count is never money, and never changes a balance. It moves with the deals:

- **A buyer's Beans go into trust:** the count drops by what they took.
- **A deal is cancelled, by either of you:** those items come back. A post that was paused stays paused.
- **A deal is released:** they are sold, and stay out of the count.
- **An admin settles a deal that went wrong** (see "When a deal goes wrong"): a refund puts the items back; a release, or a split, counts them as sold.

## Changing it

Open your post and tap **Edit Post**. You see the same boxes, and the count reads **HOW MANY DO YOU HAVE LEFT?** When buyers are holding some, a line says so: **3 are held for buyers now. They are not counted here.** Type how many you have left apart from those.

- You can change the count and the price for all of them while deals are under way. They are your stock, and each deal keeps the figures it was made at.
- You cannot change the price for one while a deal is under way, as for any Offer. Decline or cancel the deal first.
- A price for all of them is for one number, the count you saved it with. If buyers have taken some since, so it no longer fits, its box opens empty with a line such as **Your price for all of them was for 20. There are 17 left, so it is not showing. Set a price for the 17 left, or leave this empty.** Leave it empty and it comes back by itself if those deals are cancelled. A price you set now is for the count you save with it.
- If a buyer takes some while you are editing, saving stops with **Someone took some while you were editing. There are 17 left now. Check the number and save again.** Nothing of that save was kept.
- A count of 0 is refused: **To stop selling, pause or remove the listing.** You can use **Pause Offer** as for any Offer.
- You cannot delete a post while one of its deals is held. The app says so; complete or cancel the deal first.

## On the global community

A copy of an Each post on the global community shows the price for one only, such as **300 Beans each in Byron**. It shows no count and no price for all of them, because those change by the hour at home. Nobody can take it up there, as with any copy. To buy, someone asks to join your community.
