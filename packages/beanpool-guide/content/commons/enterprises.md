## What they are

Projects and enterprises are the groups that hold Beans. Each has its own beans, its own Offers and Needs, and its own credit line.

On the global community there are no enterprises, so **Commons** shows an example one from a local community, the Example Valley Bakery, marked **🏘️ Local community**. Its buttons, such as **Pledge** and **Manage**, say why enterprises live in a local community, and change nothing. See "Beans and the global community".

- A **project** has a leader, a goal in Beans and usually an end date: raise 500 Beans for a new roof, build the shade house by November. The **leader** runs it; **collaborators** help. When the goal is met, the pledges go into the project's account and it takes no more. When the work is done, its leader winds it up. After 7 days the leader finalises it, and what is left goes back to the Commons.
- An **enterprise** keeps going and trades: a tool library, the egg flock, a market stall. Its **keepers** run it together and stand behind it. One of them is the **lead keeper**.

An enterprise has its own daily limits, ten times a member's: 1,000 payments, 300 people it has never paid before, 1,000 deals approved, 1,000 new posts and 50,000 changes. What a keeper does for it counts against the enterprise, not against the keeper's own. It also counts against what that keeper does for all the enterprises they keep together, which has the same limits: however many enterprises you keep, you can do one enterprise's worth for them in a day, on top of your own. So an enterprise with two or more keepers can still use all of its limits. Once an enterprise has used up one of its limits for the day, what you do of that for it counts against your own limits instead, as if you had done it for yourself. If you then reach one of yours, the app says so and names the enterprise. So one keeper who uses up its day can only stop themselves: every other keeper still has all of their own. Running the enterprise always counts against the keeper's own changes: taking on, removing or replacing keepers, stepping down, pausing, winding up, and completing or turning down its deals.

## Finding them

Tap **Commons**, then **Groups**, and pick **Projects** or **Enterprises**. Each shows its status, purpose and balance. On the phone, the ones you keep are first under **Yours** (lead first, each with its role and a **Manage** button to run it). Tap one to open it.

## What an enterprise page shows

An enterprise's page is its shop front. Every member sees, from the top:

- its banner photo across the top, or a plain block with its category's emoji when it has none, then its logo, its name, its category and since when it has been here;
- what it is for;
- who keeps it (**Kept by**; **Run by** on a project), each with their trust level, stars and how many trades they have done. These badges show what each person has done. They don't decide anything;
- a project's goal and its pledge box;
- **About**: what its keepers say about it;
- **Featured**: up to six of its Offers its keepers chose to show first;
- **What we do together**: the kinds of thing its keepers offer;
- its listings, in sections: **To buy**, **Book a time**, **To borrow** and **Looking for**. Tap one to open it;
- **How we deal**;
- **Message**, which opens a chat with its lead keeper (its leader on a project);
- its balance and credit line in one line. Tap **Money and backing** to see its live Offers, surplus and capital ceiling, what its keepers back it with, and **Income & Spend**: every bean in and out. Its keepers see who paid or was paid and the note with it; other members see the amounts, and their own payments in full;
- **Recent activity** and a **Discussion** you can write in.

Where a community lets people who are not members read its pages, they see its logo, name and category, what it is for, its keepers' names, About and a project's goal, but not its banner photo, listings, badges or money. Most communities don't: there, only members can open the page.

At the bottom, **Report Enterprise** (**Report Project** on a project) if something is wrong.

## Backing an enterprise

- **Pledge beans to a project:** on its page, under the funding box, enter an amount and tap **Pledge Beans**. You can make or change up to 20 pledges in any 24 hours, a keeper's backing included, and a pledge of beans is one of your payments too. Pledges are held in trust until the project reaches its goal, and then they all go to the enterprise. After that it takes no more pledges, and nor does an enterprise that is winding up or has closed: its page says so where the pledge box was, and your beans stay with you. If a project is cancelled before it reaches its goal, every pledge goes back to the person who made it.
- **Trade with it:** its Offers and Needs are in the Market like anyone's.
- **Become a keeper:** see "Keepers".

## If you keep it

If you keep an enterprise, or help run a project, everything only its keepers do is under **Manage**: at the top of its page on the phone, and in the web app its page has two tabs, **Page** and **Manage**. So its page shows what every member sees. A community admin sees **Manage** too. **Manage** shows only what you can do:

- requests to join, keeper changes waiting and, when the lead has gone quiet, choosing a new one. See "Keepers".
- **Its shopfront:** its name and what it is for, its logo, its banner photo, **About**, its category and **How we deal**. Its category is one of the Market's categories, the same ones every listing has. Any keeper can change the logo (a photo or one of the ready-made pictures), the banner photo, **About**, the category and **How we deal**. Only the lead keeper, the only keeper or a community admin can change its name and what it is for; other keepers see them with a line saying so. A community admin who doesn't keep it can change its name, what it is for, its logo and **About**, not its banner photo, category or **How we deal**. The logo and the banner photo are two pictures: the logo is the round one (a photo or one of the ready-made pictures), and the banner photo goes across the top of its page. Under **Banner photo**, tap **Choose a banner photo** (**Change the banner photo** once it has one), or **Remove the banner photo** to go back to the plain block. With a photo, for the logo or the banner, move it and zoom it until the part you want is in the frame (a circle for the logo, a strip three times as wide as it is tall for the banner, as its page shows them), then tap **Done**. Change what you want, then tap **Save** once. In the web app, **Save** sends only what you changed, and if the community says no, it says why.
- **Featured** (its keepers only): pick up to six of its Offers to show first on its page. An Offer must be in its shop first: **Put them in its shop** puts its live Offers in. If it has no shop yet, **Open its shop** opens one.
- **Operator controls:** **Post Offer** and **Post Need** post in its name (a new Offer starts in its shop's category, which you can change before you post), and **To Commons** sends some of its surplus to the Commons. Bids on its Needs and its deals are here too, to approve and to pay. In the web app, **Set map pin** puts it on the map.
- **Decisions:** its keepers' votes.
- Removing a keeper, and stepping down.
- **Season & lifecycle controls:** pausing and closing it (below).
- **Discussion:** remove a message that breaks your community's rules. It then shows as removed.

**Season & lifecycle controls** shows only the buttons you can use:

- **Pause for Season:** any keeper. Its listings pause so nobody can buy, and its credit line stays where it is, for up to 90 days. **Resume Enterprise** (**Resume Project**) ends the pause.
- **Start Wind-Up:** the lead keeper, the only keeper or a community admin. A 7-day wait starts; nothing closes the enterprise by itself. Any keeper or a community admin can tap **Stop Wind-Up** during the wait.
- **Finalise Wind-Up:** once the 7 days are up, the lead keeper, the only keeper or a community admin finishes closing it. See "Rules and how decisions work".

Before a project reaches its goal, its leader can also cancel it (on the phone, under **Manage**), and every pledge goes back.

## Starting one

- On the web or phone, tap **Commons**, then **Start something** (on the phone, the round **+** button) and pick **Project** or **Enterprise**. (On an empty list of projects or enterprises, the button takes you straight there.)
- Give it a **Name** and a **Purpose statement**.
- For a project, set a **Funding goal** (**Goal Amount** in the web app) and, if you like, a deadline.
- Add its logo if you like: choose a photo or one of the ready-made pictures (the round picture people see beside its name). With a photo, move it and zoom it until the part you want is in the circle, then tap **Done**. Its banner photo, across the top of its page, is chosen later under **Manage**.
- Tap **Start the project 🌱** or **Start the enterprise 🏛️**.

It starts straight away, and you are its leader (a project) or its lead keeper (an enterprise). There is no community vote to start one.

You can start 3 projects or enterprises in any 24 hours, and have 20 running at once. Past that the app says no. If you have 20 running, wind one up before you start another.

Its keepers change its logo, its banner photo and its words under **Manage**, in either app (see "If you keep it").
