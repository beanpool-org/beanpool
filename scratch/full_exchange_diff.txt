   7: R:   6 | +  * local and synchronous-ish, which is what makes both halves testable on one node.
   8: R:   7 | +  *
   9: R:   8 | +  * THE FOUR ENTRIES (§2.1). Buyer B on BRISBANE buys a 5-bean service from seller S on BYRON:
  10: R:   9 | +  *
  11: R:  10 | +  *     BYRON                                    BRISBANE
  12: R:  11 | +  *       S                     +5.000            B                     −5.075
  13: R:  12 | +  *       bridge_brisbane       −5.000            COMMONS_POOL          +0.075
  14: R:  13 | +  *                                               bridge_byron          +5.000
  15: R:  14 | +  *       ─────────────────────────────           ─────────────────────────────
  16: R:  15 | +  *       node total             0.000            node total             0.000
  17: R:  16 | +  *
  18: R:  17 | +  * Two invariants the code below exists to preserve, and which the tests assert directly:
  19: R:  18 | +  *   • the bridge entries are equal and opposite and carry the PRICE ONLY — never price plus fee. The fee
  20: R:  19 | +  *     is a local matter and must not cross the border.
  21: R:  20 | +  *   • the seller receives EXACTLY the agreed amount. They quoted 5, they get 5. So every transfer here is
  22: R:  21 | +  *     explicitly fee-exempt and the fee is moved by hand — the automatic 1.5% deducts from the *recipient*
  23: R:  22 | +  *     (§8), which is the local convention and the wrong one here (§2.1).
  24: R:  23 | +  *
  25: R:  24 | +  * WHO WRITES WHAT (Rule 3a). Each node writes only its own ledger. Brisbane debits its own member on that
  26: R:  25 | +  * member's own signed instruction; Byron pays its own seller under its own cap. Neither ever asks the
  27: R:  26 | +  * other to touch a member account, so a compromised peer's maximum reach is exactly the cap its
  28: R:  27 | +  * counterparty chose.
  29: R:  28 | +  *
  30: R:  29 | +  * ORDER (Rule 3c). Byron never credits its seller before it holds a signed receipt. The one reachable
  31: R:  30 | +  * failure state is Brisbane owing outward for something not yet delivered — recoverable, and never an
  32: R:  31 | +  * unbacked local mint.
  33: R:  32 | +  */
