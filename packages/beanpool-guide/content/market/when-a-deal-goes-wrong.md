## Talk first

Most problems are a misunderstanding. Tap **Message** on the post and talk it through.

## Cancelling

- **Before it is agreed:** the person who asked taps **Withdraw Request**. The person who posted a Need taps **Deny**, then **Decline**, and can add a short message. A request nobody answers ends by itself after 7 days.
- **While the beans are held:** either of you can tap **Cancel Trust Hold**, then **Yes, Cancel**. All the held beans go back to the person paying, and the post goes back on the market. The other person is told.
- **A standing deal:** nothing is held, so there is no hold to cancel. Either of you can tap **Stop…** at any time, then **Stop it**. The week or month already paid stays paid, and nothing more is taken. If the next one could not be paid, the button says **Stop now**, and it ends at once with nothing owed through the app. See "Standing deals".

In the web app, **Withdraw Request** is **Cancel Offer**, and **Deny** declines at once, with no message. To call off a held deal there, tap **Cancel Hold**, then **OK**.

Either person can cancel alone, at any time before the beans are released. That includes after the work is done. If that happens to you, talk to them, and then to your community's admins.

## When you cannot agree

There is no "dispute" button in the app. Ask the admins of your community for help. They look after its server, and they can settle a stuck deal in one of three ways:

- pay the seller (the normal 1.5% fee applies);
- give all the beans back to the buyer;
- split them half and half.

An admin cannot settle a deal they are part of, or one involving an enterprise they keep.

These settle held beans. A standing deal holds none, so an admin has nothing to settle in it. If you paid a month and got nothing, tap **Stop…** so no more is paid, talk to them, leave a fair review, and report them if you need to.

You both get an alert ("There is news on one of your trades.") and a line in your chat saying how it was settled. The chat line names the admin by their callsign, or says "a community admin". The alert itself names no one.

## If someone is not honest

- Leave a fair review. Low stars reduce the trust they earn.
- Report their post, or block them. See "Reporting a problem" and "Blocking someone".
