## Where you can send one

- **In a chat with one person**, once it is an ordinary chat: not while your first message is still a message request.
- **In a group's chat**, where it is locked end to end.
- On the phone, and in the web app in Chrome, Edge and Safari.

Voice messages can't be sent in event chats or enterprise chats (the community's server can read those), in a message request, in the forum, or in a chat with someone from another community. The microphone shows only where your community's server takes voice messages: an older server doesn't, and then there is no microphone. If that changes while you are recording, the recording is kept and the box says "This community doesn't take voice messages yet. Nothing was sent."

A voice message someone sends you in a message request shows as "🎤 Voice message: shown when you accept". It isn't fetched until you accept.

## Recording on the phone

When the box is empty, the send button is a microphone.

- **Hold it to record, and let go to send.** The microphone turns red and a clock runs while you record.
- **Slide left to cancel** ("Slide to cancel"). Nothing is sent.
- **Slide up to lock**, and you can let go. Tap stop, then play it back, delete it with the bin, or send it.
- Shorter than a second sends nothing, and "Hold to record" shows above the box.
- A recording can be up to 3 minutes. At 3:00 it stops by itself and waits for you to send it.
- With a screen reader on, or with text at 1.5 times or larger, there is nothing to hold: tap to start (**Record a voice message**), then tap stop.

The first time, holding the microphone only asks the phone to let BeanPool use it. It never asks before then. If the microphone is off for BeanPool, the app says "The microphone is off for BeanPool" with **Open Settings**.

Switching to another app stops a recording and keeps it for you to send or delete: it is never sent by itself. Leaving the chat throws away a recording you haven't sent. If a voice message can't be sent, the app says "Voice message not sent" and keeps the recording, so you can try again.

## Recording in the web app

Click the microphone to start, and stop when you are done. Then play it back, delete it, or send it. The first click asks your browser to let the site use the microphone. If the browser refuses, the web app says "The microphone is off for this site. Allow it in the browser's site settings, then try again." Shorter than a second sends nothing: "Too short to send. Record for at least a second."

- **Firefox** can play voice messages but can't record them. In its place the web app says "This browser can't record voice messages. Chrome, Edge, Safari and the phone app can."
- **A community's page opened over plain http**, as on a home network, can't record either: "Voice messages can't be recorded on this address. The phone app can."

## Listening

- Tap play. Drag along the wave to move through it.
- The speed button goes 1×, 1.5×, 2×, and remembers your choice.
- When one ends, the very next voice message in the chat plays by itself, if it isn't yours and you haven't played it. Only one plays at a time.
- On the phone it plays through the speaker, even when the phone is set to silent. It stops when you leave the chat, or switch to another app. In the web app it stops when you leave the chat.
- Nobody is told that you listened. The two blue ticks still say a message was read.

On the phone, your list of chats shows "🎤 Voice message"; so do a chat card on Home and a quoted reply, in both apps. An alert says only "You have a new message." (in a group, "New message in one of your groups.").

Voice messages can't be edited. You can delete one of yours, as any message: see "Messages".

If a voice message won't play, it says why: "This voice message can't be played." (in the web app, "This voice message can't be played in this browser." where the browser can't play the sound), or "Voice message unavailable. Tap to try again."

## How long it is kept

Your community's server keeps a voice message for 30 days, then deletes the sound. The message stays in the chat.

- **On the phone**, the sounds you sent or played are kept on the phone, up to 200 MB, the oldest going first. A voice message you played there still plays after the 30 days.
- **In the web app**, nothing is kept in the browser. After 30 days, and on a phone that never played it, the message says "This voice message is no longer on the server."
- A voice message reported in a group's chat is kept for the moderators while the report waits.

Voice messages count towards the photos you can send in a day (100 MB a day unless your community sets another amount). Past it, the app says "You've shared a lot of photos today. Try again tomorrow."

## What is private

A voice message is locked on your phone or in your browser before it is sent, with the same keys as a photo in that chat, and signed by you. Your community's server can't listen to it. It knows how big it is, which tells roughly how long it is, and who sent it to whom, and when. Its length and its wave are inside the lock.

You can report a voice message as you report any message (see "Reporting a problem"). Your community's moderators can't play a reported voice message in Settings yet: they see that it was reported, who sent it and your reason.

Deleting your account deletes every voice message you sent from your community's server. See "Leaving or deleting your account".
