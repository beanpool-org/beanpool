## Why

Beans are for using, not for piling up. A small fee on large balances keeps them moving around the community. It goes to the Commons, for everyone.

## How much

Only beans you **hold** above 200 pay. Each part of your balance pays its own rate, per month:

- the first 200: nothing;
- from 200 to 500: 1%;
- from 500 to 1,000: 1.5%;
- from 1,000 to 2,000: 2%;
- above 2,000: 2.5%.

A balance below zero pays nothing. Nobody pays interest on debt.

For example, holding 600 beans for a month costs about 4.5 beans: nothing on the first 200, 3 on the next 300, and 1.5 on the last 100.

## When

The fee builds up day by day and is taken a little at a time, whenever your account is next used. You do not lose a lump on one day.

## Seeing it

On the **Ledger**, **Wallet**, the **Community Circulation** box shows about how much you will pay this month. It only shows when you hold beans. Above 1,000 beans it turns amber and suggests spending some.

Tap it for **System Circulation**, which explains the fee.

Holiday Mode does not pause the fee.
