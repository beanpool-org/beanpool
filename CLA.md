## How to sign

You sign by writing a comment on your pull request on GitHub, when the CLA assistant asks you to. The comment is:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub username, the date and time of your comment, and the version of this document you agreed to are kept by the CLA assistant as your signature. You sign once; it covers Your present and future Contributions to the Project. If you contribute on behalf of a company, or your employer has rights in your work, section 4 applies: make sure you have permission before you sign.
