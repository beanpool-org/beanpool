## Pins

Tap **Map** in the row of tabs. The map shows your community's posts:

- **Green pins:** Offers.
- **Orange pins:** Needs.
- **Purple pins with a calendar:** events that have not finished yet.

A pin shows the post's category picture. Where many pins are close together, they join into a circle with a number. Events join the same circles as Offers and Needs, and the number counts them too. The circle is blue on the phone and dark grey in the web app.

People and groups are not on the map. In the web app, an enterprise that has set a place shows as a square pin with its picture. Tap it to open the enterprise.

Polls are not on the map either. The one exception is on the phone, in the global community, where a poll can show as a pin at its poster's area. It looks like an orange Need pin, and shows only under **All**.

## Tapping a pin

- **An Offer or a Need:** a card shows the title and who posted it. Tap **View Details** to open it. On the phone, tap their name to see their profile. Tap the map or **✕** to close the card.
- **An event:** a card shows when and where, and how many are going. Tap **View event**. In the web app the button is **View Details**, and you can also tap **Going** or **Interested** on the card.

## Tapping a circle

A tap on a circle zooms in until its pins part. The zoom stops with a few streets still in view, so you are never left too deep to find the other pins.

If the pins are on one spot, or too close together for that zoom to part them, the tap opens a list instead, with how many are there at the top. On the phone that means within about 30 metres, and about 40 metres in the web app. Events come first, soonest at the top, then Offers and Needs, newest first. The list shows only what the filters show.

Tap a row to open that post. Tap **✕** or the map to close the list. The map stays where it was.

## Filters

On the phone, at the top of the map:

- **All**, **Offers**, **Needs** or **Events**. Tap **✕** to clear.
- **All Categories** to show one category.
- For events: **All**, **Today**, **This weekend** or **Next 7 days**. The map moves to fit them.

In the web app the map has only the event date chips: **Today**, **This weekend**, **Next 7 days** and **All events**. They show only while your community has an event coming up.

## Buttons on the left

- On the phone, the moon or sun: a dark or light map.
- The target: show where you are. The first time, the app asks for permission to use your location.
- **+** and **−**: zoom.

The amber circle shows the area your community serves.

## Adding a post from the map

Tap **+ ADD POST**. In the web app, tap the round **+** at the bottom right. See "Posting an Offer or a Need".
