## What it is

**Learn** is where your community keeps its **Local knowledge**: short pages about this place, written by members. How recycling works at the tip, who to call when the road floods, where to catch the bus. An owner or admin checks every page before anyone else sees it.

Learn is on a local community, where its owners have it switched on. On the global community it shows a few example pages from Example Valley, because anyone can join there with 12 words and nobody can check who knows a place. Join a local community to use it.

## Opening Learn

Learn has no tab of its own. You reach it from:

- **Home:** the **Learn something local** card. Tap a page, or **See all**. The **Your community** card also has a line such as "6 things worth knowing" that opens Learn. See "Home".
- **The Market:** type words in the search box. When Learn has pages that match, one line sits above the results, such as **📖 In Learn: 2 for "tip" ›**. Tap it to open Learn with your words already searched. Pages of Local knowledge are never mixed in with the Market's listings.
- **The Pulse:** when a new page goes into Learn, the Pulse shows a small card, **New local knowledge**, at most one a day. Tap it to open the page. See "The Pulse".
- **Add post:** choose **📖 Local knowledge** to write a page (see below). In the web app it is a button in the New Post panel.

Tap **Back** to go back to where you were.

## Finding a page

Learn is sorted by topic, never by date: 🚨 Emergencies, 🚰 Local services, 🚌 Getting around, 🌿 Growing and animals, ⛑️ Health and help, 🏞️ Places and history and 💡 Good to know. Each topic shows up to three pages, the ones that helped most members first; tap **See all** for the rest. The chips along the top show one topic at a time.

Type in **Search Learn** to search the titles, the words, the places and the tags. If nothing matches, tap **Search the Market for "…" ›** to look there instead. On a page, tap a tag to search Learn for it.

On the phone, Learn also works without a connection: it shows the pages your phone already has, with "Couldn't reach your community; showing what we had." The web app needs a connection.

## Reading a page

A page puts the answer first: its topic, the title and what to know, then **Where:** if it is about a place, its tags and its photos. Under **Sources** are the web pages where you can check it. Each shows which site it is on, and opens in your browser.

- **Checked** and a month: when an owner or admin last confirmed the page. **Checked over a year ago**, in amber, means nobody has confirmed it for more than a year.
- **Written by**: the member who wrote it. Tap them to see their profile. A page an owner or admin put together with their AI assistant says **Gathered with the assistant from the sources above. Checked by** and their name.
- **Written in** and a language: the page is in a different language from the one you use BeanPool in.
- **This may be out of date.**, in an amber box: the writer, an owner or an admin thinks part of it may have changed. Read the note under it, and check the sources.

Every page ends with the same line: "Local knowledge is written by members. Check the source before you rely on it."

## This helped

If a page helped you, tap **👍 This helped**. Tap it again to take it back. The page shows how many members it helped, such as **Helped 12**, and never who. You can't tap it on your own page.

## Local guide

A member who wrote 3 pages that are in Learn now, each of which helped at least 3 members, shows **🗺️ Local guide**: on the **Written by** line of their pages, and on their profile. Pages put together with an AI assistant don't count. It goes again if their pages go, or if Learn is switched off. It is only a thank-you: it gives no Beans and lets nobody do anything more.

## Thanking the writer

Under a page someone else wrote, tap the button with their name, such as **🫘 Thank Ada**. It opens the Ledger's ordinary form for sending Beans, with the writer chosen and a note such as **Thanks for "Bus to town"** filled in. Change the amount or the note, then send it as any gift of your own Beans. Nobody else pays anything. The button shows only where your community uses Beans, and not on a page put together with an AI assistant. See "Sending a gift".

## Suggesting a page

Tap **＋ Suggest knowledge** at the bottom of Learn, or choose **📖 Local knowledge** in **Add post**. Write about how something works, where to go or who to call: never about a private person, and always in your own words.

- **Title** and **What to know** are needed: put the answer first, then the details. Choose a **Topic**.
- **Where**, links under **Where can people check this?** (up to 5), tags and a photo are optional. Add only your own photo: without one, the page shows a picture for its topic.
- Tap **Send to the admins**. Nobody else sees it until an owner or admin has checked it.

You can have 3 suggestions waiting at once, and send 5 in a day. To suggest one, your profile needs a name and a photo, as for posting in the Market.

The answer comes to you as a notice: **Your local knowledge is in**, or **Your local knowledge wasn't added**, with the reason and any words the owner or admin added. A notice never says who answered.

## My suggestions

When you have something waiting, Learn shows a line such as **1 of yours is waiting for an admin ›**. Tap it to see **My suggestions**: each one says **New** or **Fix** and since when it has waited. Tap **Take it back** to withdraw one before it is answered. Answered ones stay in the list for 30 days, marked **Added** or **Not added** with the reason.

## Something changed?

On any page, tap **Something changed? Suggest a fix**. Say under **What changed?** what is different now, such as "The tip is closed on Mondays since March", and change the words if you like, or send the note alone. An owner or admin checks it first: the page stays as it is until then. You can have one fix waiting for each page. You hear back as **Your fix is in** or **Your fix wasn't used**.

The writer of a page can also tap **Mark as out of date** and say why. That puts the amber box on the page until an owner or admin clears the mark.

## For owners and admins

- What you write yourself goes into Learn at once: the button reads **＋ Add local knowledge**, then **Publish**. The Pulse announces it.
- **To check** is in the web app: Learn shows a line such as **3 to check ›**, and the bell says when a member suggests something. Open a suggestion to see it as members would. For a fix, you see what changed beside the page as it is now. Then **Approve**, **Edit first**, or **Not this one** with a reason. Approving a new page adds it to Learn and announces it in the Pulse. A draft from an AI assistant waits until you tick **I opened the sources and they say this.**
- On any page: **Edit** changes it at once, **Still right** confirms it as it is and sets **Checked** to this month, and **Mark as out of date** or **Clear the mark** puts the amber box on or takes it off.
- **To check** also lists the pages nobody has confirmed for a year, under **Not checked for a year**.
- To take a page down, open Settings: under **People & Safety → Triage & Moderation**, choose **Local knowledge** in the posts list's **Type Filter** and delete it.

On the phone, To check comes in a later update: use the web app for it.

## Something wrong with a page

Tap **🚩 Report** at the bottom of the page and choose why, as for any post. See "Reporting a problem". The people who run your community look at it, and an owner or admin can take the page down.
