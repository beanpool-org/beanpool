## Poll or Decision?

A **Poll** asks what people think: "Market on Sunday or Saturday?". It changes nothing by itself. For something binding, propose a Decision.

## Starting a poll

- Tap **+ ADD POST** on the Market tab or the Map.
- Choose **Community Poll**.
- Write the **Question**.
- Add **Context / Notes** if it helps. This is optional.
- Write 2 to 4 **Options**. Tap **+ Add Option** for more.
- Choose how long it runs: **3 Days**, **7 Days** or **14 Days**.
- Tap **Create**.

You can have one poll open at a time, and a community can have 5 open at once. You need credit that is not frozen to start or answer a poll.

## Finding and answering polls

Polls are in the Market. Tap **Polls** in the top row to see only polls.

- Tap an answer to choose it. Tap another to change your mind, until the poll closes.
- The card shows how many chose each answer.

Polls are not secret. **Your vote is visible to members**: tap **View voters** to see who chose what. A member who is suspended sees only how many chose each answer, until the suspension ends.

## Closing a poll early

If you started it, tap **Close Poll**, then **Close Poll** again to confirm.
