## Opening it

Tap **Ledger** at the bottom of the screen. Your balance is at the top right, in **BEANS**. It can be below zero; see "Your credit line".

The Ledger has two tabs: **Levels** (your trust, see "Trust badges") and **Wallet**. Tap your balance to go straight to Wallet.

## The Wallet tab

- **Your balance**, and roughly how many hours of help it is worth, at 40 beans an hour.
- **Commons:** your community's shared pool.
- **Credit Floor:** how far below zero you could go.
- How many of your beans are held in deals that are under way, when there are some.
- **Community Circulation:** shows when you hold beans. See "The circulation fee".
- **Project pledges:** beans you have pledged to projects, if any.
- **Send Credits:** send a gift. See "Sending a gift".

## Recent transactions

Every trade, gift and fee, newest first, with who it was with, a note, the time, and plus or minus. If a note mentions a post, tap **View Offer** to open it.

The list has no search or filters yet.

## ⓘ buttons

Tap the **ⓘ** next to Your balance for a short explanation of your balance and your floor.
