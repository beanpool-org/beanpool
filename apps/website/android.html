BeanPool for Android, as a download

For Android phones without Google Play, or where Google Play is hard to reach. Most people should use Google Play.

This is the same app as on Google Play, signed by the same key. It installs over the BeanPool already on your phone, whether that came from Google Play or from here, and keeps your account.

Download

Download BeanPool 1.2.83

Version 1.2.83, a file of 166 MB: use Wi-Fi if you can. It comes from BeanPool's releases on GitHub.

Install it

  1. Open the file once it has downloaded: tap it in the notification, or find it in your phone's Downloads.
  2. The first time, Android asks you to let your browser install apps. Allow it in the settings that open, then go back.
  3. Confirm the install. If BeanPool is already on your phone, Android calls it an update. Then open BeanPool.

Updates

An app downloaded here does not update itself. BeanPool tells you when a newer version is out: come back to this page, download it, and install it over the one you have. Your account and everything in the app stay.

Keep your 12 words safe

Your account is a key kept in the app on your phone. Never uninstall BeanPool to put this one on, because uninstalling takes your account off the phone: install over it instead. Your 12 words bring your account back on a new phone. In the app, they are in Settings, under Account & Identity, at View Recovery Phrase. Write them down and keep them safe.

Check that it is ours

BeanPool for Android is signed with one key. The SHA-256 fingerprint of its certificate is:

46:AA:D0:CB:A8:9D:1F:E7:EF:F0:60:99:77:CE:06:5D:85:DD:7E:AC:13:57:D4:48:97:EC:70:AF:B2:02:6C:81

On a computer with Android's build tools, run:

apksigner verify --print-certs BeanPool-1.2.83-305.apk

It should print the same fingerprint, written the way apksigner writes it:

Signer #1 certificate SHA-256 digest: 46aad0cba89d1fe7eff0609977ce065d85dd7eac1357d44897ec70afb2026c81

Android installs an update only when it is signed with the same key, so a file signed with any other key cannot replace your BeanPool.

The SHA-256 of the file itself:

ef6112f14891ad653e7ad0d06cc0aa061fa5a97907c84b9055cfdd6229c06089

The other ways to get BeanPool

  • Google Play, for most Android phones. It keeps the app up to date for you.
  • The App Store, for iPhone and iPad.

No app at all? The global community opens in your phone's browser at global.beanpool.org.
