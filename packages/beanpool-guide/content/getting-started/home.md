## What Home is

The app opens on **Home**: a dashboard of cards answering what needs you, what is happening around you, and what you could do next. Each card is a few lines and one tap into that part of the app. A card with nothing to say takes no room on the screen.

When you first see Home, a one-line tip appears: "This is your Home. Tap … on any card to move or hide it." Tap **✕** to put it away; once closed, it never shows again.

## The cards

Home shows only the cards that have something to tell you, in order from top to bottom:

- **Needs you:** only while something is waiting for you — a deal waiting on you, an unread message, a vote closing, or admin reports to review. This card stays first and cannot be moved or hidden.
- **Your way back in:** if you joined with 12 words and have no connected sign-in, this card reminds you to keep them safe or add a sign-in. Tap **✕** to put it away; it returns after your first post and after a week, until you add a sign-in.
- **First steps:** on a local community, guides you through posting your first Offer, adding a profile photo, choosing your interests, and inviting someone. It disappears once finished.
- **Find your community:** on the global community, shows communities nearest you and lets you ask to join them.
- **What are you into?:** lets you star categories you like. It shows when you have no categories starred yet, or when you tap **Tune** on the Market card.
- **Your deals:** shows your open deals and deals waiting on you.
- **Your enterprise:** shows requests waiting to be approved for enterprises you keep.
- **Coming up:** upcoming events near you, with your RSVP.
- **New in the Market:** the newest listings (or listings near you on the global community), with starred categories first. Tap **Tune** to pick your interests, or **See all** to open the Market tab. When a community is new, it may show example listings.
- **Decide:** open Decisions in Commons and open polls in the Market.
- **Your groups:** your groups and chats, with unread message counts.
- **Who joined:** people who joined your community this week.
- **The Pulse:** posts shared by neighbours from external channels like YouTube and blogs, with starred categories first. Tap **See all** to open the Pulse.
- **Your Beans:** your balance and credit line (room to spend).
- **From your community:** announcements and notices from your community's admins.
- **Grow your community:** lets you invite new members after your first Offer, where invites are enabled.
- **Your community:** member count and trades this month. Tap **Edit home** here to customize your cards. This card stays at the bottom and cannot be moved or hidden.

## The "…" menu

Every card except **Needs you** and **Your community** has a **…** button at the top right. Tap it to:

- **Hide:** removes the card from Home.
- **Move up:** moves the card above the card before it.
- **Move down:** moves the card below the card after it.

## Edit home

At the bottom of Home, on your community's card, tap **Edit home**:

- Switch any card on or off to show or hide it.
- Tap the up and down arrows to change the order of your cards.
- Hidden cards sit under **Hidden** with their switches off so you can bring them back at any time.
- Tap **Reset to defaults** to return all cards to their original order and unhide them.
- **Needs you** always stays at the top, and **Your community** always stays at the bottom.

## What are you into?

Tap categories you like (Food, Tools, Services, Garden and others) to star them:

- Starred categories come first on Home in **New in the Market** and **The Pulse**.
- Starred categories never hide anything: other listings and posts follow right after.
- The stars you pick on Home are the exact same stars used under **★ For You** in the Market tab.
- Tap **Tune** on the Market card anytime to change your stars.

## On the web lobby

Before you join or sign in on the web, a visitor sees a simpler Home:

- A **Join** card at the top.
- Only public cards: **Find your community**, **Coming up**, **New in the Market** (or **Near you**, with photos and categories but no member names or faces), and **Your community**.
- Visitors have no **…** menu, no **Edit home**, and no private cards like **Needs you** or **Your Beans**.

## Privacy and signing out

Your Home layout and starred categories belong to your account:

- When you sign out (or use **Remove from This Device Only** on the web), your Home cards, layout, starred categories, and cached answers leave the phone or browser completely.
- A new account, or an account on another device, starts with its own layout and stars.
- In the web app, signing out in another tab clears Home immediately from the browser.
