## What is in it

In the **Pulse**, tap **Learn**. It holds:

- videos from BeanPool about how it works;
- the **Daily Reflection**, a short thought that changes every morning. It is just a heading; there is nothing to open.

Members can add their own how-to channels to Learn too, by choosing the subject **How it works** when they add a channel.

## Videos are extra

Everything you need is written in this guide. The videos are there if you would rather watch.

When a guide page has a matching video in your community's Learn lane, the page shows **Watch:** and the video's name at the top. Tap it to open the video. If there is no match, or you are offline, the link simply does not show.

## Why a video might not show

- Your phone is offline, or cannot reach your community's server.
- Your community's server has not picked up a new video yet.
- There is no video for that page yet.
