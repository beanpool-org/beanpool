Your community's owners and admins can connect their own AI assistant (for example Claude or ChatGPT) to your community's server, to help them look after it: to ask what needs them, or to summarise the open reports. They connect it themselves, and they can disconnect it at any time. The other owners are told when one is connected.

## What it can read about you

An assistant sees much less than an admin does in Settings. Your community's server decides what it gets, not the assistant.

- **If someone reports you or your post**, and the admin asks for the report's words: the reason given, your post's title and description, and your BeanPool name. Never who made the report.
- **If you ask to join**, and the admin asks for the messages: the name and message you sent with your request, and the community you said you came from. Never your photo or your key.
- **Totals for the whole community**, such as how many members there are and how many Beans are in circulation. Never your balance.

## What it never sees

Your messages and chats, your balance and trades, your keys, your address, your photos, the names list, and who reported anyone. It can't change anything about you: it can't remove you, mute you, take down your post or answer your join request. An admin does those, in Settings.

## Who receives it

What the assistant reads goes to the company that runs it, as when you paste something into an AI chat yourself. The server sends a report's words or a join message only when the admin's assistant asks for them, marked as words from a member and not instructions.

If you'd rather your report or your join message wasn't read by an AI, ask your admins how they use theirs.
