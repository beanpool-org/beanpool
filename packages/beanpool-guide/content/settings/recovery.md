## On a new phone

Your account's key is not in your phone's backups. A new phone needs your 12 words or a sign-in account you linked, even an iPhone restored from a backup of your old one.

- Install BeanPool and open it.
- Tap **Already a Member? Restore Account**.
- Choose **Recover with 12 Words** or **Recover with Social**.

## With your 12 words

- Type the 12 words in order, or tap **Paste**.
- Type your community's name or address.
- Tap **Recover Identity**. Your name and photo come back from your community.

## With a sign-in account

This works only if you linked one before (see below). BeanPool's key vault keeps the locked copy, and your sign-in finds it: you don't type your name or your community's address.

- Tap **Recover with** and the account you linked: Google or Facebook, or Apple on an iPhone.
- With Google on an iPhone, a Google web page opens. Sign in there and choose the account you linked. On Android, Google shows its own list of accounts. If it can't show one on that phone, a Google web page opens instead.
- For your safety, getting back in with a sign-in waits a day. The app says when your account comes back. Keep BeanPool on this phone: it checks by itself while it is open, and **Check now** asks straight away.
- If you still have another phone with your account on it, open BeanPool there and tap **Yes, it's me** (see "If someone else tries"). Your account comes back the next time this phone checks, in about a minute. BeanPool in a web browser can't do this: only the phone app can.
- When your account is back, choose the community to open it in. The global community is filled in; to use your own, type its name or address, or tap it under **Your communities on this phone**. Tap **Continue**. You can add your other communities afterwards.
- **Use my 12 words instead** works any time, with no wait. **Start again** starts over with a sign-in.

If the app says **Getting back in with a sign-in is paused for a little while**, BeanPool's key vault has restarted and is waiting for the people who look after it to open it again. Nothing is lost: try again later. Your 12 words work any time.

If the app says **BeanPool's key vault keeps no copy for this** (sign-in) **account. Your community may still keep one.**, your copy is still at your community, from before the key vault (see "Moving a copy your community keeps"). Type your name in the community and your community's name or address, and sign in once more: your account comes back through your community's server, as before.

If the sign-in account was linked from a phone that had your 12 words, on an up-to-date app, your 12 words come back too. Otherwise the phone has no 12 words: they can't be rebuilt from a sign-in. If you have them written down, you can add them (see "Your 12 words"). Until then a linked sign-in account is that phone's way back, so keep one linked, and link a second one if you can (see below).

## Protecting your account now

- Open **Settings** and tap **Account Protection**.
- Under **Sign-In Recovery Providers**, tap **Protect with** and choose an account.
- Pass your phone's lock screen check. The linked account can bring your account back, so the app asks first.
- With Google on an iPhone, a Google web page opens. Sign in there and choose your account, and you come back to BeanPool.

One linked sign-in account covers all your communities: you don't link it again in each one. This works on a phone with no 12 words too.

If you joined the global community with 12 words, use **Add a sign-in**, near the top of **Account Protection**, instead. It is a way back too, and it also lifts the limits on a new 12-words account. See "Your 12 words".

If this phone has your 12 words, the linked account keeps them as well, so restoring with it gives them back. An account you linked on an older version of the app keeps only your key: tap **Connect again** next to it, and pass your phone's lock screen check, to include the words. On a phone with no 12 words there is nothing to include, so **Connect again** is not shown.

This is not a way to log in. Your account is still your own key. The linked account only helps you get it back.

- If BeanPool's key vault is paused when you link an account, nothing is linked and the app says so. **Settings** offers to link it again once the vault is back.
- A sign-in account protects one BeanPool account. If it was protecting a different one, it protects this one now, and the app says so: that other account then has only its 12 words.
- To remove it, tap **Disconnect**. On a new phone it then no longer brings your account back from the key vault, in any community. The key vault deletes its copy at once, and from its backups within 30 days, or later if the vault is paused in that time. **Disconnect** doesn't remove a copy a community still keeps for that sign-in: see "Who can open the copy".

## Moving a copy your community keeps

Before the key vault, each community kept its own copy for the sign-in accounts linked there. If yours still keeps one, and the key vault keeps none for that sign-in account, **Settings** shows **Move your Google recovery to BeanPool's key vault** (or Facebook, or Apple), with that community open. Tap **Move it** and sign in once, as when you link an account. The key vault gets a fresh copy, made on this phone, and your community's copy is then removed. **Not now** puts the card away for a week.

If you'd rather not keep a copy anywhere, tap **Remove it instead**, then **Remove it**. Your community's copy is deleted and none is kept at the key vault, so that sign-in account no longer brings your account back through your community or the key vault until you link it again. A copy another community still keeps for it is not touched: open that community to remove it there. On a phone with no 12 words, the app says so first: if you lose the phone after this, nothing brings your account back.

Until you move it, the old copy still works: if you lose your phone, the app finds that the key vault keeps no copy for that sign-in, and gets your account back through your community's server, as above. It then offers to move the copy.

## If you had linked GitHub

BeanPool no longer uses GitHub. A GitHub account's id is public, so a copy locked to it was not locked to anything only you have. When your community's server is updated, it deletes every copy linked to GitHub. Your account itself is not touched, and your 12 words still work.

- If GitHub was the only account you linked and your 12 words are on this phone but not written down, write them down now (**Settings**, then **View Recovery Phrase**), or link Google, Facebook or (on an iPhone) Apple, as above. Until you do one of these, this phone is your only way into your account.
- If your 12 words are written down, you have nothing to do. To have a sign-in account as well, link Google, Facebook or (on an iPhone) Apple, as above.
- If GitHub was the only account you linked, this phone has no 12 words and you have none written down, link Google, Facebook or (on an iPhone) Apple now. Until you do, this phone is your only way into your account.

## Who can open the copy

When you link Google, Apple or Facebook in an up-to-date phone app, your app locks a copy of your account with that sign-in and sends it to BeanPool's key vault: a small server in Iceland that does nothing else. No community keeps a copy made there, not even yours. The vault gives the copy back only after Google, Apple or Facebook tells it that it's you, and your other phones are told when that happens (the phone app is told, not BeanPool in a web browser). It waits a day first, so you can stop it if it wasn't you.

BeanPool can open these copies. For now one person, BeanPool's founder, looks after the vault. It is moving to three people in different countries, and then two of them must act together. The vault's backups from before that move stay until they are 30 days old, or longer if the vault is paused then. Nobody can log in to the vault. The company that hosts it can read its memory while it runs, as with any rented server. A court could order a copy opened. Anyone who takes over your Google, Apple or Facebook account could get in too, unless a phone of yours stops it during the day's wait: see "If someone else tries".

If you would rather nobody but you could ever get in, keep your 12 words written down, tap **Disconnect** next to each sign-in account, and remove each copy a community still keeps (below). **Disconnect** deletes the key vault's copy at once, and from its backups within 30 days, or later if the vault is paused in that time. It doesn't remove a copy a community keeps. Your 12 words always work, with or without the vault.

Some copies are still kept by a community's server, and the people who run that server can open them, because their server checks your sign-in:

- A copy your community kept from before the key vault, until you move it or remove it, with that community open (see "Moving a copy your community keeps"). If the key vault already keeps a copy for the same sign-in account, **Settings** offers that only after you tap **Disconnect** next to it. Copies of it already in that server's backups stay until those backups are deleted.
- On the global community, the copy kept for a sign-in you joined with, or added, in the web app, until you remove it the same way, with the global community open in the phone app. See "Using BeanPool in a web browser".

## If someone else tries

If someone starts getting back into your account with your linked sign-in account, your phones are told, and **Settings** shows **Someone is getting back into your account**, with when it goes through. Only the phone app is told, and only it can answer: BeanPool in a web browser can't.

- If it was you, on another phone, tap **Yes, it's me** and pass your phone's lock screen check. It goes through now, and **Settings** shows **Let through**: your other phone gets your account the next time it checks.
- If it wasn't, tap **Stop**. It never goes through, and whoever tried gets nothing. Then make sure that sign-in account has its own strong password.

A copy your community still keeps from before the key vault is watched as before: **Settings** shows **Someone is recovering your account** while a restore with it is under way. If it is not you, tap **Stop It Now**: your community's server then hands nothing more to that restore. Moving the copy to the key vault (above) gives it the day's wait too.

## If the phone already has an account

Restoring a different account replaces the one on this phone, whether you restore with your 12 words or with a sign-in account. The app asks first, on **Replace this phone's account?** (with a sign-in account, when you tap **Continue**, or once you have signed in if your community's copy brings it back). Nothing changes until you type **WIPE**, tap **Replace Account** and pass your phone's lock screen check. Tap **Keep**, then the account's name, **go back** to leave everything as it was.

Make sure you have the 12 words for the account you are replacing. If this phone has them, that screen shows them when you tap **Show** (the account's name) **12 words** and pass your phone's lock screen check. If this phone has no 12 words for that account (Settings says so), without a linked sign-in account you cannot get it back.

## Lost your phone and your 12 words

Without your 12 words and without a linked sign-in account, an admin of your community can move your account to a new phone, with your Beans, your name and your history. Go and see them in person: they check it's you first.

- The admin makes a re-key code on their screen. If your old phone still has the app, it gets a notice that an admin started moving your account.
- On your new phone, open BeanPool, tap **Already a Member? Restore Account**, then **An admin gave me a re-key code**. Scan the QR on the admin's screen, or type the code (it starts RK-) and your community's name. In a browser, the QR's link opens the same screen; or open your community's web app, tap **I'm Already a Member →**, then **An admin gave me a re-key code**, and type the code.
- Your phone shows a two-digit number, and under it the address of the community it asked. Show both to the admin: they type the number on their screen. The number makes sure it's your phone they bind, and not someone who saw the code. **Not now** leaves the number: your phone keeps its place, and the same code shows the same number again.
- If the new phone had no BeanPool account, it then shows the 12 words of your **new** key. Write them down: your old words no longer work, and your old phone stops working for good. A phone that already had an account (for another community) keeps that account's 12 words, and they now bring this account back too.
- If you had linked a sign-in account, your phone asks you to link it again in Account Protection: the one you had protected your old key, so it was removed.

The code lasts 24 hours. If your admin says your phone isn't the one they saw, or the code runs out, ask them for a new one.
