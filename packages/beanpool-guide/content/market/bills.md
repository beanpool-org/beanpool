## What a bill is

A bill asks one member to pay you a number of Beans: who it is from, what it is for, and how many Beans. **A bill never pays itself. Nothing is ever taken by itself.** The person it goes to chooses whether to pay it, and can say no.

- **What it is for:** 3 to 80 characters, in your own words.
- **How many Beans:** more than 0, and 2,000 at most.
- It is not a deal. Nothing is held in trust, there is no review, and a paid bill builds nobody's credit line. Trust and credit are earned on the Market, in deals.

BeanPool and your community never send bills. A bill always names the member who sent it.

## Bills in the app today

For now, the one place to send a bill is an invite: you can add a bill for the person you invite (below). The person who joins can't see the bill in the app yet, so they can't pay it yet either. It waits for them. See "Coming soon" at the end of this page.

## Adding a bill to an invite

- Open **Invites**: on the phone, the person with a plus at the top right; in the web app, **People**, then **Invites**. See "Joining BeanPool".
- Under the note (and under **Vouch for them**, if you are a voucher), tap **Add a bill for them (optional)**. Two boxes open: **What is it for?** and **How many Beans?**
- Under them it says what you would receive, such as **You receive 59.10 of 60 Beans: 1.5% goes to the community.**, and **They choose whether to pay, once they have joined. Nothing is taken by itself.**
- Tap **Generate Ticket** (in the web app, **Generate New Invite**). The bill goes with the invite only while the fold is open: close it and the invite goes without a bill. What you typed stays, in case you open it again.
- One bill per invite. A bill can't go on a ticket made without a connection (**Generate Offline Ticket**). If no answer comes back, the app says **No answer came back, so it isn't known whether the invite was made. Try the same bill again when you're online: if it was made, you'll get that invite, not a second one. A bill can't go with an offline ticket.**
- On the phone, an invite for a name on the names list has no bill fold.

**What they see.** When you share the invite, the message says **Rob has also sent you a bill to pay in Beans once you're in.** It never says how much. Joining never shows the bill and never waits for it: nothing in joining asks about it.

**While nobody has used the invite.** Under **Pending** the invite says **bill 60 Beans**. If you revoke it, the app says **Whoever has the link can't use it, and the bill that went with it is withdrawn.** An invite nobody uses in its 30 days ends, and its bill ends with it.

**Once they join.** The bill is theirs from the moment they join with that invite, and waits 30 days from that day. A vouch on the same invite gives them credit, but it can't pay your bill: a first bill from you can only be paid with Beans they hold, until the two of you have finished a deal (see below).

## Paying a bill

These are the rules your community's server keeps for every bill.

- Paying moves the Beans at once and for good. It can't be undone.
- The 1.5% community fee comes off what the sender receives, as in any deal. A bill of 60 Beans gives the sender 59.10, and 0.90 goes to your community's Commons.
- **A first bill uses Beans you hold.** If you and the sender have finished a deal on a listing (either of you paying), or have a standing deal running, you can pay their bill from your credit line, as far as your live Offers open it (see "Your credit line"). Otherwise, a bill from them can only be paid with Beans you hold: it can never take you below zero.
- Saying no is quiet. The sender is not told: their bill reads "not paid" until it expires.
- A bill waits 30 days from reaching you, then expires. Until it is paid, the sender can withdraw it.
- The app never reminds you about a bill. A bill from someone you have never added, written to or traded with makes no alert and adds nothing to any count.
- A bill from someone you have blocked is never shown to you, and can't be paid.
- If either of you deletes your account, or is removed from the community, the bills between you end, and nothing is paid.

## Limits

So that nobody can flood people with bills, in any 24 hours one member can send:

- 10 bills;
- of them, 3 to people they have never finished a deal with. A bill on an invite is one of these 3.

And at any time:

- 3 bills to one person in 30 days that aren't paid. One they said no to counts, and so does one withdrawn, so the limit never tells you a bill was declined.
- 20 bills out that aren't paid.

Over a limit, the server says when you can send more, such as **You've sent 10 bills today. More from 16 Nov, 14:30 UTC.** An enterprise's limits are ten times a member's.

If three different members report one person's bills within 7 days, that person's bills are put on hold: they can't send one, and none of theirs can be paid, until the owners or admins have looked at the reports. The owners are told.

## Good to know

- **Enterprises.** An enterprise can't pay bills yet, so nobody can send one a bill.
- **Where there are bills.** Bills need Beans. The global community and a community without Beans have no bills, and **Add a bill for them** isn't there.
