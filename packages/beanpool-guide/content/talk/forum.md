## Where it is

Tap **Chat** in the row of tabs. Where your community has a forum, the top of **Chat** is a switch: **Chats** and **Forum**. **Chats** holds your messages and groups, as before. **Forum** is the forum. Where your community's owners have switched the forum off, or its server is older, there is no switch and **Chat** looks as it always has. It is the same on the phone and in the web app.

Every community has a forum unless its owners switch it off, in Settings under **Core settings**. When they do, its topics are kept for when it is on again, and members are told on Home: "Your community's owners changed how it works: the forum is off. Its topics are kept for when it is on again."

On the phone you can also add a **Forum** card to Home with **Add a card**: it shows the newest topics, with **Open the forum ›**. The web app has no Forum card yet.

If this phone has also joined the global community, the forum of each of your other communities shows **Worldwide forum ›**. Tap it to switch to the global community's forum. The web app doesn't have this row.

## Sections and topics

A forum has sections, and each topic sits in one of them. **All sections** at the top picks the section to show, or all of them. Pinned topics come first, then the topic with the newest reply. Each topic shows how many replies it has, and **New replies** marks a topic you follow that has replies you haven't read.

The global community's forum starts with these sections: **News from BeanPool**, **Introduce yourself**, **Start a community near you**, **Skills & how-to** and **Ideas for BeanPool**. In **News from BeanPool** only the owners and admins start topics; anyone can reply to them. A local community's forum starts with **General** and **Ideas for our community**.

## Starting a topic

Tap **＋ New topic**. Choose a **Section**, write a **Title** (up to 100 characters) and **What do you want to say?** (up to 5,000), and, if you like, a **Place, in words (optional)** such as "near the town hall" (up to 80 characters). A place is only words: it is never a pin on the map. You can add up to 3 photos, made at most 800 px on their longest side. Then tap **Post topic**.

## Replying

Open a topic and write in **Write a reply…** at the bottom, up to 3,000 characters, then tap **Send**. The photo button adds up to 3 photos (**📎** in the web app). Replies are listed under the topic in the order they were written, 30 at a time: **Show more replies** brings the next ones.

- **Answering one reply:** tap **⋯** on it, then **Reply**. Your reply quotes it, as a chat does. **Don't quote** takes the quote off.
- **Mentioning someone:** type **@** and their name, up to 5 people a post.
- **Links:** a web address in a post can be tapped, up to 5 a post.
- **On the phone**, a reply you haven't sent stays in the box, with its quote and photos, the next time you open the topic.

Every member can start up to 10 topics and write up to 100 replies in any 24 hours. Owners and admins have their community's own limit instead. Posts you removed still count.

There are no voice messages in the forum.

## A post's menu

Tap **⋯** on a post for **Reply**, **Copy**, **Report**, **Block** and the person's name, and on your own posts **Remove**.

- **Remove** asks first: "Remove this post? Its words and photos go for everyone." The post then reads **Removed by its writer**.
- Removing the opening post of your own topic: if nobody else has replied, the whole topic goes. If others have, its title and opening words go, it reads **A removed topic**, it is closed, and their replies stay.
- The apps have no **Edit** yet. To change what you wrote, remove it and write it again.

The **⋯** at the top of a topic has **Mute this topic** and **Close this topic**. A topic can be closed by its writer, an owner or an admin. A closed topic can still be read, and says "This topic is closed, so it takes no new replies."

## Following a topic

You follow a topic when you start it or reply in it. The bell at the top of a topic follows a topic you have only read (**Follow this topic**), and tapping it again stops (**Following: tap to stop**). **Mute this topic** keeps a topic you are in quiet.

- A reply in a topic you follow makes one row in your bell and one alert: "There is a new reply in a topic you follow." More replies add to the same row until you open the topic, so a busy topic doesn't fill your alerts. A reply alerts at most 200 of a topic's followers, those most recently active in it first.
- When someone mentions you, the alert says "You were mentioned in the forum.", at most once an hour from the same person.
- These alerts have their own switch: **Forum** ("Replies in topics you follow, mentions, and first topics") in your notification settings, on the phone and in the web app. See "Notifications".

## Searching

**Search the forum** finds topics and replies that have every word you typed, newest first. You can search up to 100 times in any 24 hours. Search never shows a post that was removed or hidden, a topic waiting for a first look, or posts by someone you blocked.

## New on the global community: your first topic

On the global community a new account starts gently:

- You can reply at once.
- Your first topic waits for a moderator to look at it before anyone else sees it. The form says so: "Your first topic is looked at by a moderator before it shows.", with how many hours it usually takes, worked out from the last 20 first looks. While it waits, the forum shows "Your first topic "…" is waiting for a first look.", with **Withdraw it** to take it back. One topic can wait at a time.
- When a moderator puts it up, your bell says: "Your first topic "…" is up in the forum. From now on your topics go up at once." If they don't, you are told why, never who looked. If nobody looks within 30 days, it comes back to you, and you can start it again.
- While your account is new, photos go only in that first topic, where a moderator sees them first. Links in your posts show as plain words, and mentioning someone doesn't tell them.
- You can start 2 topics and write 10 replies in any 24 hours (1 and 5 if you joined with 12 words).

Your account stops being new when its first days are over (72 hours and 3 posts still up; 7 days if you joined with 12 words), when a moderator has put one of your topics up, or when you are given a role. Forum posts that are still up count among those posts. On a local community there are no first looks: every topic goes up at once.

If you are an owner, admin or moderator on the global community, **Waiting for a first look** with a number shows at the top of the forum. Open it and tap **OK, put it up**, or **No** and then the reason (**Why not?**): **Spam or a scam**, **Offensive content**, **Misleading**, **Unsafe or illegal** or **Against this community's rules**, then **Say no**.

## Who can read it

- **A local community's forum** is for its members. Nobody else can read it.
- **The global community's forum** can be read today by the global community's members. Before your first post there, a message says "Anyone on the web can read this forum, with your name. Not your picture, your area or your profile.", with **Post** and **Not now**. The forum's own pages on the web are not built yet: you are told now, so that nothing you write becomes public by surprise when they come. The server takes no first post until you have seen it.
- **It is not locked like a chat with one person.** As with the Market, your community's server holds the words and photos, and the people who run it can read them. Your community's owners, admins and moderators can read every section.
- You never see posts by someone you blocked. A topic of theirs you had open says "You blocked {name}, so their topic no longer shows for you." with their name. See "Blocking someone".
- Deleting your account takes the words and photos off every post you wrote; each then reads **Removed by its writer**. See "Leaving or deleting your account".

## Reporting a post

Tap **⋯** on the post, then **Report**. "The community's moderators see this post and your reason." The reasons are the same as everywhere else. See "Reporting a problem".

On the global community, a post that enough people report is hidden until a moderator looks; others read **Hidden while moderators look**. A moderator then removes it, and it reads **Removed by a moderator**, or shows it again. If it is your post, you are told each step, and the reason if it is removed. An account that has three posts removed by the moderators within 30 days is muted, where the community uses the automatic mute: while muted it can't start topics or reply, but it can still remove or close its own.
