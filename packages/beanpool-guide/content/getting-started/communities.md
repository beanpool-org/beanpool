## One account, several communities

Your phone holds one account: one name, one key, one set of 12 words. You can use it to join more than one community. Each community keeps its own beans, trades and posts.

## Joining another community

- A community lets you in with an invite from one of its members. No invite? From the global community you can ask a community near you to let you in: see "Finding a community near you".
- Tap the invite icon (a person with a plus) at the top right of the screen to open **Invites**.
- Under **Join Another Community**, paste the invite link or code. If you only have a code, also fill in the community's address.
- Tap **Join Community**.

Your key, your 12 words and your name come with you. Your posts, chats and trades stay in each community. Tap **Next**, then check your name and photo for the new community. A sign-in account you linked in an up-to-date phone app, to get back into your account, works there too: BeanPool's key vault keeps one copy for all your communities.

## Joining the global community from your account

The global community needs no invite, and you join it with the account you already have: the same key and the same 12 words. The app offers it only when the global community is open.

- Tap the bean at the top left. In the **BeanPool** sheet, under **Your communities**, tap **Join the global community**. It is also in **Settings**, under **Advanced / Subsystem**. If you are visiting the global community as a guest, tap **Join** at the top, beside the **?**, then **Join with a sign-in** (**Join with 12 words or a sign-in** where the community takes the 12 words).
- Choose how to join: **Create an account with 12 secret words**, which uses the 12 words your account already has (no sign-in, so no phone lock), or **Or sign in** once with Google, Apple (on an iPhone) or Facebook. For a sign-in, the app asks for your phone's lock first, because this sign-in also becomes a way back into your account.
- Check the name people there will see, and tap **Join**. If someone already has that name, the app suggests others.
- Then choose your photo for the global community, as for any new community, and then roughly where you are (**Your Area**, which you can skip): the global community shows you the listings near it. See "Joining BeanPool".

Nothing changes in your other communities, and your 12 words stay the same. A sign-in also becomes a way back into the same account: BeanPool's key vault keeps a locked copy of it for that sign-in, so if you lose your phone, it brings back this account, not a new one. If the key vault can't keep one just then, you still join, and you can link that sign-in later under **Account Protection**. See "Getting your account back". A sign-in you linked to your account before keeps working. If you join with 12 words and no sign-in keeps a copy of your account, the app shows the **Your account has one way back** card, as for anyone who joins with 12 words (see "Joining BeanPool").

If the app says **This sign-in already joined the global community with a different BeanPool account**, that sign-in has its own account there. Use a different sign-in: one sign-in account makes one account in the global community.

On a new phone with no account yet, use **Explore BeanPool worldwide** on the first screen instead. See "Joining BeanPool".

## What the global community shows you

The global community has listings from everywhere, far more than a phone needs to hold. So your phone keeps the ones near you:

- the nearest 500 listings within 250 km of your area, the rough place you gave when you joined. See "Your area";
- your own listings, and any listing you have a deal in progress or a conversation about, wherever they are.

The Market's list and the Map on your phone show what your phone keeps. New listings near your area come in as they are posted. If you change your area, your phone swaps in the listings near the new one.

Nothing far away is out of reach:

- **Search** in the Market asks the community, so it finds listings from everywhere, not only near you. Without a connection, it searches what is already on your phone.
- **A listing you open** from a message, a link or Home is fetched from the community, wherever it is. If the community no longer has it, it says **Post not found**.
- **The Map** shows only what your phone keeps, so far from your area it has few pins or none. Search the Market to find things there.

Without an area, your phone keeps the newest 100 listings from everywhere instead, and a line above the Market says **You're seeing the newest listings worldwide. Set your area to see what's near you.** Tap it to set one. You can still post, message and search as before.

A poll has no place of its own, so it is shown at the area of the member who made it: the polls you see are mostly ones made near you.

In the web app, the Market and the Map ask the community each time: for the listings near your area or near a place you pick, or else the newest from everywhere. See "Finding things near you".

Your other communities work as before.

## Switching

- Tap the bean at the top left. The **BeanPool** sheet opens, with **Your communities** near the top.
- Tap the one you want.

You can also switch in **Settings**, under **System**, then **Advanced / Subsystem**. Your communities are listed there. The one you are using is marked as active. Tap **Switch to Town** on another one to move to it.

## Forgetting a community on this phone

- In **Advanced / Subsystem**, tap **Forget** on a community you are not using right now. This removes it and its saved data from this phone.
- In the **BeanPool** sheet, under **Your communities**, pressing and holding a community and tapping **Remove** only takes it off the list. Its data stays on the phone.

Neither deletes your account there, and your other communities are not affected.

To delete your account in one community, open it and use **Permanently Delete Account** in Settings. The phone keeps your key for your other communities. See "Leaving or deleting your account".

## Visiting as a guest

If you add a community without an invite, you are a guest there. A **Join** button shows at the top, beside the **?**. A guest sees very little until they register with an invite. In the global community, which has no invites, a guest joins with their 12 words or a sign-in instead: see "Joining the global community from your account" above.
