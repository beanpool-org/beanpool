## Before you can

- You need to have completed a trade in the market. A trade of only 1 or 2 beans is too small to count.
- You can only give beans you hold. A gift can never take you below zero.

Until then the button says what is missing, like **needs 1 completed trade** or **needs positive balance**.

Your trust badge does not matter.

## Sending

- Tap **Ledger**, then **Wallet**, then **Send Credits**.
- Tap **Select recipient...**, search for their name and tap it.
- Type the **Amount**. Add a **Memo** if you like, such as "Thanks for the tomatoes".
- Check **Recipient receives**. Gifts have no fee, so they get it all.
- Tap **Confirm Transfer**. You see **Sent!**

A gift cannot be taken back. Check the name before you send.

## Good to know

- Gifts do not build trust. Only completed trades do.
- You can send gifts while on holiday.
- You can make up to 100 payments in any 24 hours. Gifts, buying, asking to buy an Offer, approving help on your Need, pledges of beans to a project and buying from another community all count. A keeper's backing of an enterprise moves no beans, so it is not a payment: it counts only as one of your 20 pledge changes a day. Up to 30 of them can go to people you have never paid before; paying people you have paid before still works. Receiving is never limited. If you reach a limit, the app says which, and when you can pay again.
