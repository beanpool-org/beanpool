R:   9 | +  *
R:  10 | +  *     BYRON                                    BRISBANE
R:  11 | +  *       S                     +5.000            B                     −5.075
R:  12 | +  *       bridge_brisbane       −5.000            COMMONS_POOL          +0.075
R:  13 | +  *                                               bridge_byron          +5.000
R:  14 | +  *       ─────────────────────────────           ─────────────────────────────
R:  15 | +  *       node total             0.000            node total             0.000
R:  16 | +  *
R:  17 | +  * Two invariants the code below exists to preserve, and which the tests assert directly:
R:  18 | +  *   • the bridge entries are equal and opposite and carry the PRICE ONLY — never price plus fee. The fee
R:  19 | +  *     is a local matter and must not cross the border.
R:  20 | +  *   • the seller receives EXACTLY the agreed amount. They quoted 5, they get 5. So every transfer here is
R:  21 | +  *     explicitly fee-exempt and the fee is moved by hand — the automatic 1.5% deducts from the *recipient*
R:  22 | +  *     (§8), which is the local convention and the wrong one here (§2.1).
R:  23 | +  *
R:  24 | +  * WHO WRITES WHAT (Rule 3a). Each node writes only its own ledger. Brisbane debits its own member on that
R:  25 | +  * member's own signed instruction; Byron pays its own seller under its own cap. Neither ever asks the
R:  26 | +  * other to touch a member account, so a compromised peer's maximum reach is exactly the cap its
R:  27 | +  * counterparty chose.
R:  28 | +  *
R:  29 | +  * ORDER (Rule 3c). Byron never credits its seller before it holds a signed receipt. The one reachable
R:  30 | +  * failure state is Brisbane owing outward for something not yet delivered — recoverable, and never an
R:  31 | +  * unbacked local mint.
R:  32 | +  */

R: 155 | + /**

R: 169 | +     privateKey: any,

R: 175 | +         throw new SettlementError(`${key} is not ours to commit`, 'wrong_direction');

R: 179 | +         key: row.key,
R: 180 | +         issuerPeerId: ourPeerId,

R: 185 | +         amount: row.amount,
R: 186 | +         committedAt: row.state === 'committed' ? row.updatedAt : new Date().toISOString(),
