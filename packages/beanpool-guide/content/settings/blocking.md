## Blocking

- On their profile: tap **Block**, or **Block** and their name lower down.
- On one of their posts: tap **Block User**.
- From a chat: tap their name at the top to open their profile, then **Block**.

Confirm with **Block User**.

## What it does

- Their posts, Offers and messages are hidden from you.
- They disappear from your People list and your chats.
- A report goes to your community's admins, who can look into it.

## What it does not do

- They are not told.
- They can still see your posts.
- The block is kept on your phone. If you sign out, reinstall the app or use another device, block them again.

## Unblocking

- Open **Settings**. Under **Legal & Privacy**, tap **Manage Blocked Users**.
- Tap **Unblock** next to a name, or **Unblock All**.
