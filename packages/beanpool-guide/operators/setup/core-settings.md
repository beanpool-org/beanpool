**Core settings** is the last item in the left menu. It holds the settings your members joined under, the ones that are hard to take back, and the ones that are money. You set them once, when you start your community, and then you lock them. After that, changing one is deliberate: you unlock it, and Settings asks for your proof.

Only an owner can confirm, lock and unlock. Admins can read every setting, its reasons and the log, and can advise the owners about a setting, but cannot change anything. Moderators and members don't see this page. Members keep their own "How this community works" facts on Home, and are told when a rule changes.

## What is a core setting

- **Who can join**: **Invite: any member invites**, or **Known: only admins invite**. On a global server the door is open to anyone and the server holds it. Pausing new members is not here: that is an everyday switch under **People & Safety → Invites & QR**, and needs no unlocking.
- **12 words at the open door** (global community only): whether a stranger can join with 12 secret words alone, or needs a sign-in.
- **Beans**: the medium of trade, credit and the Commons. Once any member has traded and the ledger has moved, Beans are locked on and cannot be turned off.
- **Known floor for confirmed members**: how far confirmed members on the names list may go into debt while keeping an offer listed. Needs Beans on.
- **Community votes (Decisions)**: formal proposals and votes, on or off.
- **Members may share listings outside the community**: on by default. A member can share one listing or event with someone outside BeanPool: the link shows that one post for 7 days, under the rules a visitor gets (what it is, its photos, its price and its area; never a name, a face or the exact place), and leads to your community's own card and Ask to join. Turned off, every link already shared stops at once and stays stopped if you turn it on again. Always off during a private preview. Your server keeps only a fingerprint of each link, never the link itself.
- **Where members' copies go**: when a member links Google, Apple or Facebook, where the locked copy of their account is kept: BeanPool's key vault, this server, or nowhere.

At the top, **Kind of server** (local or global) is read-only: whoever runs the server sets it.

## Confirm and lock

Your community runs on its starting choices from its first second; nothing waits for you. Until you confirm, Settings offers the walkthrough in four places: an item under **Action Required** on Home, a **!** beside **Core settings** in the menu, a banner at the top of the page, and a line on the owner's card on your phone. It never blocks anything and has no deadline.

- Press **Walk through them**. Each step is one setting, with the choice your community already has chosen, what each choice means, and what changing it later would do. **Back**, **Next** and **Leave for now** are on every step; nothing is saved until the last step, and leaving saves nothing.
- On a local community the first step after the introduction is **Where your community is**. It is required: a local community confirms its core settings only with its place set. Search for the town or street and pick it; place the pin and set the radius exactly later, under **Appliance & Data → Node Identity**. A global community needs no place.
- At **Review**, check the list, then press **Confirm and lock**. Settings saves the place first, then the choices that changed, then the lock, as one step. A setting the server holds (the global community's open door, Beans off by kind, a ledger that has moved) is listed as **Set by this server**.

A community that already has members opens the walkthrough at the review, with today's values filled in, and **Confirm and lock** changes nothing: it only locks. **Walk through them** goes back to the first step.

Once locked, the page says **Locked since ‹date› by ‹name›**, and every owner is told.

## Unlock to change

Each core setting has its own **Unlock to change** button. There is never an unlocked mode and no way to unlock them all at once.

- Press **Unlock to change** on the setting. The setting opens, with what changing it will do.
- Make the change. For the three one-way changes you also type a word before **Save** works: **off** (Beans off), **stop** (sharing outside off) or **nowhere** (copies go nowhere).
- Press **Save**. Settings asks your proof when you save: you pressed **Manage** on your phone within the last 90 seconds (if not, it says **Press Manage on your phone again, then make this change within 90 seconds**). A computer signed in with your phone's key is told to do it from the phone.
- The change is saved with a **Unlocked** line in **Changes**, every owner is told, and members are told in the usual words. The settings are locked again at once.

Everything the server refuses stays refused, and its own line says why. Beans cannot be switched off once anything has traded. Beans cannot be switched on while the open door is set.

## Where the everyday rules went

These are everyday rules, so they need no unlocking: **Pause new members** and **Requests to join** are under **People & Safety → Invites & QR**; **First message from someone new**, **Limits on new accounts**, **Reported posts hidden and message requests cleared automatically** and **Repeat offenders muted automatically** are under **People & Safety → Triage & Moderation**; **Listed in the BeanPool directory**, **Share location & radius**, **Share member count**, **Share email**, **Share phone**, **Share health**, **Visitors** and **Private preview** are under **Appliance & Data → Node Identity**; **Connections to other communities** is under **Appliance & Data → Gateway & Peers**.

## Advising the owners

An admin sees **Advise the owners** under each core setting, before the lock and after it. Write up to 200 characters about that setting and press **Send**. It changes nothing and needs no proof. Every owner gets an alert that an admin advised about that setting, and your words are kept in **Changes** as "Advised the owners about ‹setting›", where owners and admins read them. The alert carries neither your words nor your name, because alerts can leave the server for a chat or phone channel. An owner answers by changing the setting, or by leaving it as it is: there is no reply thread. Each person can send up to 10 pieces of advice in a day.

## Changes

The log at the bottom lists every change to the core settings: who, when, what changed, any note to members, each lock and unlock, and each piece of advice to the owners.
