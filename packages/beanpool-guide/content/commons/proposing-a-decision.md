## Before you start

- A Decision is for something your community's server can carry out: removing or suspending a member, freezing or unfreezing someone's credit, choosing who may vouch, or paying beans from the Commons. For anything else, start a Poll instead.
- You can propose once you have completed a trade, or an admin has given you a trust badge. A vouch alone is not enough.
- You can have one open Decision at a time.

## Proposing

- Tap **Commons**, then **Decide**, then **+ Propose a Decision**.
- Choose what it is about: **Member** or **Commons Pool**.
- **Title:** one line, like "Grant 200 beans to the Tool Library".
- **Description & Case:** explain why, in at least 10 characters. Say it plainly. People vote on what they read here.
- From the list below that, choose exactly what should happen. For example, for a member: Remove Member, Suspend Member or Freeze Credit. For the Commons: Grant to Enterprise, Hardship Grant or Write Off Deficit.
- Fill in who it is for, and how many beans if there are any.
- Tap **Submit Community Decision**.

Voting opens at once and runs for 7 days.

## If the app says no

- **Complete a Trade First:** you cannot propose yet.
- **Limit Reached:** you already have an open Decision. Wait for it to close.
- **Error:** check your description is at least 10 characters long.
