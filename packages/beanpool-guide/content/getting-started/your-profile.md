## Change your name, photo or bio

- Open **Settings** with the sliders button at the top of the screen.
- On your card at the top, tap **Edit**, or tap your photo.
- Change what you like:
- **Callsign** is the name people see.
- **Bio** is a few words about you, up to 200 characters.
- Tap the photo to change it.
- Tap **Save Profile**.

You can also tap **Re-run Setup** in Settings. It walks you through your name and photo again, and the short tour of how BeanPool works.

## A name and a photo come first

You need a name and a photo before you can post or accept a trade. If one is missing, the app says **Finish your profile first**. Tap **Set up profile** to add it.

## Contact details

Under **Contact details** you can add a phone number, an email or a WhatsApp number. You do not have to.

Once you add one, choose **Who can see this?**:

- **Hidden:** nobody.
- **Trade Partners:** meant for people you trade with. For now it works like Community: every member can see it. To keep it private, choose Hidden or Friends.
- **Friends:** people you have added as friends.
- **Community:** every member of your community.

## What others see

Tap the round picture at the top right of the screen to see your own profile. Other members see much the same:

- your name, when you joined and your bio;
- your trust badge, how many trades you have done, with how many people, and how many you finished;
- star ratings and reviews from people you traded with;
- your posts.

## Your working style

On your profile there is a short, optional quiz, **Take 60s Quiz**. It describes how you like to work with others. On someone else's profile it shows how well your styles fit. It changes nothing else.
