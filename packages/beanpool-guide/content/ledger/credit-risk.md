## What it is

**Credit risk** is one word that the people who give credit here (the owners, admins and vouchers) see beside a member's credit line. It helps them decide whether to give someone more. It is advice for a person. It is not a score, it is not a trust badge, and it is not on your profile.

The word is one of four, each with a shape so it never rests on colour alone:

- ○ **Credit risk: not known yet.**
- ✓ **Credit risk: low.**
- ! **Credit risk: medium.**
- ✕ **Credit risk: high.**

Under the word are the reasons, each one sentence. The worst rule that holds sets the word, so one reason at high makes it high however many are low.

**Not known yet is not a bad mark.** It is what a newcomer reads, and it means start small.

You can read what the givers see about you: **What people who give credit see**, on your Ledger.

## What it is built from

Every fact is on your community's own server. Three kinds:

- **Facts any member can already read on a profile:** when someone joined and who invited them, and how many of their trades were completed and cancelled.
- **The balance and the credit line:** how much of the line someone is using. Every giver sees this. For 30 days it also counts debt that a gift of Beans paid off (see below).
- **Facts only owners and admins see:** whether they are confirmed, who vouched for them and how much, whether an admin froze their credit line, whether their trades look like a ring, a dispute an admin decided against them, when they last made a sale, and how the people they stood behind are doing.

The figures in the reasons below are examples.

## The reasons, by level

### High

Any one of these makes it high.

- **An admin froze their credit line.** Owners and admins read this.
- **They owe 900, 100 more than their credit line allows.** Every giver reads this. The line was lowered or frozen after they spent.
- **Spending paused: they owe 900, more than the 300 their Offers unlock.** Every giver reads this.
- **Their trades look like a ring: the same Beans going round a small group.** Owners and admins read this.
- **They owe 900 of 1,000, almost all of it, and have made no sale for 60 days.** Owners and admins read this. Almost all means nine tenths or more.
- **An admin decided a dispute against them in the last 12 months.** Owners and admins read this.
- **Fewer than half of their trades were completed.** Every giver reads this. It counts once 4 or more have ended, as a profile does.
- **Credit they stood behind was written off.** Owners and admins read this.

### Medium

- **They owe 900 of 1,000: more than half.** Every giver reads this.
- **Below zero (900) with no sale for 60 days.** Owners and admins read this.
- **2 of the 4 people they stood behind are below zero.** Owners and admins read this. It needs at least 2, and at least half.
- **Only trades inside their own circle so far.** Owners and admins read this. Their circle is the person who invited them, the people they invited, who vouched for them and whom they vouched for.
- **3 trades cancelled.** Every giver reads this. It needs 2 or more, and fewer than 8 in 10 completed.

### Low

None of the above, and a reason to trust:

- **Confirmed as known by an admin.** Owners and admins read this.
- **5 completed trades with 3 different people outside their own circle.** Owners and admins read this. It needs 3 trades with 2 people at least, and 30 days here.

A balance above zero is never a reason to trust. A friend can send Beans for an afternoon, and for 30 days a gift does not clear what someone owed either.

### Not known yet

None of the above.

- **New here: no completed trade yet.** Every giver reads this.
- **2 completed trades so far.** Every giver reads this.

## When a gift of Beans paid the debt

If someone owed Beans and another member sent them Beans as a gift, the balance on their row is the real one, straight away. The word waits. For 30 days it still reads what they would owe by their own trading, until their own sales have earned it back. Then the five reasons built from what someone owes read like this.

At high:

- **By their own trading they would owe 900, 100 more than their credit line allows. A gift of Beans paid 900 of it in the last 30 days.** Every giver reads this.
- **By their own trading they would owe 900, more than the 300 their Offers unlock. A gift of Beans paid 900 of it in the last 30 days.** Every giver reads this.
- **By their own trading they would owe 900 of 1,000, almost all of it, and they have made no sale for 60 days. A gift of Beans paid 900 of it in the last 30 days.** Owners and admins read this.

At medium:

- **By their own trading they would owe 900 of 1,000: more than half. A gift of Beans paid 900 of it in the last 30 days.** Every giver reads this.
- **By their own trading they would be below zero (900), with no sale for 60 days. A gift of Beans paid 900 of it in the last 30 days.** Owners and admins read this.

A gift to someone who owes nothing is not counted, so nobody's word gets worse because they were sent Beans. A sale, a refund, a paid bill or a grant from the Commons is not a gift. A deal they take that is then cancelled within the 30 days earns nothing back: the Beans that went into it and came back count as if they had never moved. The reason never says who sent the Beans or when.

## What a voucher sees

A voucher sees the same word as an owner or admin. They read the reasons any member could read and the reasons built from the balance and the line. Where a fact only owners and admins see moved the word, they read one more line:

- **An owner or admin can say more.**

So a voucher who looks at someone who owes 900 of 1,000 and has not sold for 60 days reads high, then **They owe 900 of 1,000: more than half.**, then **An owner or admin can say more.** They are never told about a freeze, a ring, a dispute or who stands behind someone. If a friend then sends that person 900 Beans, the voucher reads the same word and the same reason in the form above, with how much the gift paid.

## Half, 60 days, nine tenths and 30 days

"More than half" and "60 days" are your community's own two lines, the ones the owner sets and the join screen states. If an owner moves them, the reasons move with them. Almost all of the line is nine tenths.

A gift of Beans goes on counting for 30 days. Your community's list of members who owe more than half reads what they owe now; for those 30 days the word also counts debt a gift paid.

## How it can be gamed

Said plainly, because the people who give credit should know where the word can be wrong:

- **Friends trading to look established.** Three small trades with two friends can read low. Only trades with people outside someone's own circle count, and the ring check looks for the same Beans going round. A ring spread across unrelated accounts still passes. It is expensive, not impossible.
- **Topping up before asking.** A friend sends Beans, and the row reads Balance 0 or plus 50, as it is. The word does not follow the gift: for 30 days it reads what they would owe by their own trading, and says how much a gift paid. A gift is not a sale, so their last sale does not move, and a balance above zero is never a reason to trust. A gift can't be sent back from a balance of zero: the way back is a purchase, which puts the debt and the word back as they were.
- **A new key wipes a bad record.** Cancellations and disputes stay with the old account. A new account reads not known yet. It reads low only once an admin confirms it, or after real trades with people outside its circle over thirty days. A confirmation is one person on one entry of the names list, refused over an open debt, with the admin's name on it for the owners and admins.
- **A friendly voucher or a forwarded invite.** Confirmed is only as good as the admin who confirmed. A forwarded invite for a named person confirms whoever uses it. The card that owners and admins see names who stands behind someone, so they can judge that too. A voucher is not told who. For someone an admin confirmed, a voucher reads Credit risk: low, the reason any member could read, and "An owner or admin can say more."
- **Waiting it out.** Thirty days is cheap. It only moves not known yet to low together with real trades.
- **A figure invites false precision.** Minus 40 is small on a line of 2,000 and nearly everything on a line of 50, so a balance always comes with the line beside it.
- **Spending fast to be topped up.** A voucher vouches their whole budget to friends, asks for more and is refilled. The ask carries how their people are doing, a voucher can ask once a week, and the ceiling of 2,000 and the limits on one person hold.
- **Looking, not giving.** Every look is recorded with who looked and at whom, and the credit committee can read how many people a voucher looked at beside how many they vouched for. Stopping a voucher's budget ends their sight at once.
- **A gift dressed as a sale or a bill.** The friend buys an Offer or pays a bill instead of sending a gift, and that counts as the member's own trading. It costs the 1.5% market fee each way, leaves a deal or a bill on record, and the circle and ring checks read the deal. It is expensive, not impossible.
- **Parking the Beans for 30 days.** The friend leaves the Beans there for the whole 30 days, and the word clears. By then it is a month's loan that can't come back as a gift, and the member has owed nothing for a month.
- **Paying your people's debts before asking.** A voucher sends Beans to the people they stood behind, so that "2 of 9 below zero" reads 0 of 9 when they ask for more. That count is a plain count of who is below zero now. It costs the voucher real Beans they can't take back, and each of those people's own word still counts the gift.

## What it does not do

Your community's server enforces nothing from this word. The limits stay the limits: the most a person can owe, a voucher's budget, how much one voucher can stand behind one person, and the rule that a member who is below zero cannot be vouched for.

A gift that lifts someone to zero does let them be vouched for again: that rule reads the balance as it is.
