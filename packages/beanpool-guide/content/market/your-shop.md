## What a shop is

Your shop is a front window on your own account. It holds the Offers you put in it, under one name, with a few words about it. Beans from them are yours, as for any Offer. It is not an enterprise: nobody else keeps it, and it shows nothing your Offers don't already show.

You have one shop in each community you are in.

## Opening it

Open **Your shop** from the Home card **Your shop and tools**, or from **Tools**. To put the card on Home, use **Edit home** and add **Your shop and tools**.

Tap **Open my shop**. Until you give it a name, it is called after you: "Ana's shop".

## Its name, kind and words

Each of these is one field. Fill them in any order, or not at all:

- **Name**: up to 60 characters. Leave it empty to be called after you again.
- **Kind**: one of fourteen, such as **Tools and equipment**, **Repairs** or **Food from the garden or farm**. It helps neighbours find you and picks the tools shown. It changes nothing else.
- **About**: what it is, in a few words.
- **How I deal**: where to pick things up, and when they come back.

## How members see it

Tap **See it as members do** on Your shop. It shows your shop as other members here see it: its name, kind and since when, your picture and trust level, About, the Offers you feature, its Offers in sections (**To buy**, **Book a time**, **To borrow**), How I deal, and a **Message** button that opens a chat with you. To change it, tap **Change your shop**.

## Putting Offers in it

Only your own Offers for everyone here go in a shop. A Need, or an Offer for a group or one person, stays out.

- **When you edit an Offer:** tick **Put this in your shop**, or untick it to take the Offer out.
- **Add my live Offers:** on Your shop, tick the Offers you already have up and tap **Put them in my shop**.
- **From a tool:** when you list something from a tool, **Put this in your shop** is ticked. If you have no shop yet, it opens with that Offer. You can change its title or words on the new Offer form and it still goes in. If you change it completely, or post something else instead, it goes up outside your shop and the app tells you. To put it in, open it, tap **Edit Post** and tick **Put this in your shop**.

The new Offer form on the map has no tick yet. Post the Offer, then put it in from its edit screen or from Your shop.

## Closing it for now

Tap **Close for now**. You can say when you are back: in a week, in two weeks or in a month. Its Offers come off the Market and wait. Nothing goes in while it is closed, and none of its Offers can be put back up on its own.

Tap **Reopen my shop** to put them back. An Offer you paused yourself stays paused, whether you paused it before you closed the shop or while it was closed. One you ended stays ended, and one you took out of the shop from its edit screen stays paused, outside the shop.

In the Tool shed, **Lent to someone outside BeanPool** and **Delete** pause a thing's Offer for you while the shop is closed, so it stays off the Market when you reopen. **Take out of your shop** is not there while it is closed, but a thing you took out before you closed it stays out.

## Closing it

Tap **Close my shop**. Its Offers stay in the Market as ordinary Offers, or end too if you choose **End its Offers too**. Your private tool lists stay on your phone. You can open a new shop any time.

You can't close it, or close it for now, while a deal on one of its things is asked for or under way. The app says how many deals are open, with a button to **My Deals**.

## On the global community

A shop works the same on the global community. Someone who has not joined sees what kind of shop it is and how many things are in it, never its name or yours.

## In the web app

The web app does not show shops yet.
