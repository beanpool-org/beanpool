## Opening the Tool shed

Open the **Commons** tab, tap **Tools**, then **Tool shed**. If you put the Tool shed on your Home, its card opens it too.

The first time, it asks how you start. **I already do this** goes straight in. **I'm starting out** shows a few examples and links to this page.

## Your things

Tap **Add a thing**. Give it a name. The rest is up to you: a photo, a category, its condition, what it is worth if it is lost or broken, how you lend it, the longest loan in days, and a note only you see.

- The photo stays on your phone (in the web app, in this browser). It goes to the Market only with a listing you post.
- **If it's lost or broken** is in your own words and money, like "$120" or "like for like". It is settled between you and the borrower. BeanPool never holds or collects it.
- **Your own note** is for you: where it lives, a serial number.

The list shows your newest things first, each with where it is: **On your shelf**, **In your shop**, **Out on a loan**, or **With** the person you lent it to. **All**, **In your shop**, **Out** and **On your shelf** narrow the list.

The Tool shed has room for some hundreds of things. It counts the room they take, not how many there are, so long notes and terms take more of it. Under **Add a thing** it says about how many more fit, going by the things you have. When it is full, it says so and keeps nothing new until you delete something or shorten some notes. A community server that hasn't been updated yet keeps half as much, and the Tool shed counts the room against that.

## Lending it through BeanPool

Open a thing and tap **List in your shop**. On the phone, pick a fee first: **Free**, **Beans per day**, **Beans per week** or **A fixed number of Beans** (where Beans are off, it is free), then tap **Go on to post**. **Put this in your shop** is ticked there unless your shop is closed for now (see "Your Shop"). The Offer form opens with the thing's name, photo and how you lend it. Check it and tap **Post**. Nothing is posted before that, and if your community asks for a profile photo first, the form says so.

Then open the thing again, tap **Link it to your Offer** and pick the Offer you posted. Now the Tool shed knows it is in your shop.

Where your community's price guide has something like it, the thing says what the guide suggests a day or a week. Free is fine too.

Someone who wants to borrow it asks through the Offer, as for any Offer, and you agree it in the deal (see "A deal step by step"). While it is out, the thing says **Out on a loan**. When the deal is finished, its Offer is finished too, so the thing says **Its Offer has gone from the Market**. Tap **List it again** to post a new one, or **Forget it** to keep it on your shelf.

**Take out of your shop** pauses its Offer. **Put back in your shop** puts the same Offer back in the Market.

A thing taken out of your shop can have its Offer back in the Market without the Tool shed: you put it back up in the Market itself, or you reopened a shop that was holding it. The thing then says **Its Offer is up in the Market again**: tap **Put back in your shop** and the Tool shed shows it in your shop too. If someone borrows it meanwhile, it says **Out on a loan** and is in **Loans**.

If your shop is closed for now, nothing goes back in it until you reopen it: **Put back in your shop** says so, and changes nothing. **Take out of your shop** is not there while it is closed. A thing you lend outside BeanPool or delete while it is closed keeps its Offer off the Market when you reopen, and so does one you took out before you closed it.

## Lending it outside BeanPool

Tap **Lent to someone outside BeanPool**. Write who has it and when it should be back: in 3, 7, 14 or 30 days, or no date. If it is in your shop, its Offer is paused while it is away.

When it comes home, tap **It's back**. If it was in your shop, its Offer goes back in the Market. If your shop is closed for now, the thing comes home taken out of your shop instead: tap **Put back in your shop** once you reopen.

On the phone, the Tool shed reminds you when something lent outside BeanPool is due back. You can turn that off in the Tool shed's **Settings** (the **…** at the top).

## Loans

**Loans** lists everything of yours that is out, or that someone has asked to borrow, with anything overdue first. Tap one to open it.

## Where it is kept

The Tool shed is kept on your phone (in the web app, in this browser), with a private copy on your community's server that only you can read through the app. Like everything on that server, its operator could. You can turn the copy off in the Tool shed's **Settings**, and **Remove my data** there takes everything in it away.
