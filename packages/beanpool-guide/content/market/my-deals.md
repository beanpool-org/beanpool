## Opening it

Tap **Your deals** at the top of the Market tab. A red number on it counts the deals waiting on you.

On the global community there are no deals, so **Your deals** shows three example deals from a local community, Example Valley, marked **🏘️ Local community**: one waiting on you, one with the Beans held safely, and one done. Their buttons say why deals live in a local community, and change nothing. See "Beans and the global community".

## Three tabs

- **In Progress:** deals under way, and requests waiting for an answer. Each card says whose step is next, for example **Waiting on you: answer** (someone has asked), **Waiting on you: release** (the beans are held and the deal is done) or **You asked · waiting on** the other member. A deal with the beans held safely is marked **Held in Trust**. A standing deal holds nothing, so it is marked **🔁 STANDING** and shows the same line as its post, such as **This month's 50 went to Rob on 9 Nov · the next 50 go on 9 Dec**; while it runs well it is not waiting on you, and it adds nothing to the red number. If a period could not be paid, the card of the person paying says **⚠️ This month's 50 Beans couldn't be paid · 50 needed by 24 Nov or it ends**, and that does count. The card of the person being paid says **This month isn't paid yet · we'll keep trying until 24 Nov**, and does not count. The number on the tab counts the cards listed underneath.
- **My Posts:** your Offers and Needs that are up, or **PAUSED**. A post someone has asked for shows **Action Required**.
- **History:** finished, cancelled and declined deals. Choose **All**, **Received** or **Given**. Each shows the date, the amount and the other person. A standing deal stopped with **Stop now** says so: **Stopped by you on 12 Dec · this month unpaid · nothing owed** or **Stopped by** their name **on 12 Dec · …**. One that ended because a period was never paid says that. A standing deal that ended any other way, stopped with **Stop…**, run to its number of periods or ended because its post was removed, gets no extra line.

Tap a deal in **In Progress** or **My Posts** to open the post. That is where you accept, finish or cancel a deal, or stop a standing deal with **Stop…**. A request nobody answers ends by itself after 7 days. See "Standing deals".

## Reviews

A finished trade in **History** has a **Review** button. After you review, it says **Edit**, and you can change your stars. A standing deal asks for one review, not one for each month.

In the web app the button says **Leave Review**, and only until you have reviewed that trade. To change a review there, open the post.
