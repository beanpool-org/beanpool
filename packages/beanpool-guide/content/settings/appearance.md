## Choosing

- Open **Settings**. Under **App Settings**, find **Appearance**.
- Tap one. It changes at once; there is no Save button. The one you chose has a tick and a darker border.
- **Same as phone** (**Same as device** in the web app): **Light** when your phone or computer is set to light, and **Classic** when it is set to dark. It follows the device, even while the app is open.
- **Classic:** dark, with olive green. This is how BeanPool starts, on the phone and in the web app.
- **Light:** always light, pale and warm.
- **Dark:** always dark, charcoal and blue.

Your choice is kept on that phone or in that browser, and it is the same in every community you belong to. It is not sent to your community's server, and no owner or admin can change it for you. A phone or browser that already has a choice keeps it. The phone app used to start light, so if yours is light and you would like Classic, tap **Classic**.

Whatever you choose, the colours keep their meaning: green for money, red for danger, orange for a Need.

Under **App Settings** too, **Background pattern** puts doodles behind the app. Switch it off for a plain background. It is on to begin with, and it is kept on each device.

On the phone, the map has its own light and dark button, on the left of the map.

In the web app, the first choice is called **Same as device**.
