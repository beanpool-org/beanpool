# Photo credits

Every photograph in `photos/` is released into the public domain under **CC0 1.0** (verified on each file's Wikimedia
Commons page, licence shown as "CC0", by `prop=imageinfo` metadata on 2026-11-09). CC0 asks for no credit; it is given here anyway.
Each file is the Commons original scaled to 800 px on its longest side and saved again as JPEG. Eleven were scaled by Commons' own
thumbnail service and saved with macOS `sips` at quality 35. The other six (swapped or re-cut in round 2, 2026-11-09: firewood, lift to
the station, lawn mowing, repair café, wheelbarrow and couch) were scaled with Pillow from Commons' 1280 px thumbnail and saved at the
best JPEG quality that stays under 90 KB. Five photographs are cropped, only to take a brand name, a sign or a foreign landmark out of
the frame: the couch (a wall poster in Swedish letters), the lawn mower (the maker's name on its housing), the repair café workbench
(a stamped country name on a fitting), the wheelbarrow (a roofing maker's label on a raised bed) and the railway (an alpine slope and
a pylon above the line). Nothing else was changed.
None shows a face. They illustrate the invented "Riverbend" community in `riverbend.json`; they are not photographs of Riverbend,
and the people and listings they sit beside are made up. All 17 were looked at, on a contact sheet and then one by one at full size
(2026-11-09), for a shot of a New South Wales hinterland town in late spring: none shows snow, a readable sign or a brand name.
