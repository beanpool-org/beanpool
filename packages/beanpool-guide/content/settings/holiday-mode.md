## Turning it on

- Finish or cancel any deals that are under way first. The app will not start Holiday Mode while you have one.
- Open **Settings**. Under **App Settings**, switch on **Holiday Mode**.

## While you are away

- Your Offers are hidden.
- Nobody can send you new requests.
- You cannot post or start a deal yourself.
- Your balance and your credit line stay as they are.
- Gifts still work, and the circulation fee on large balances still applies.

## Coming back

Switch **Holiday Mode** off. Your Offers are live again.
