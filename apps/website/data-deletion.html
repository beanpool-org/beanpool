Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Your BeanPool account is a key made on your own device. What you post, trade and write lives on the servers of the communities you joined, some of which BeanPool may run. If you linked a Google, Apple, GitHub or Facebook sign-in, a locked copy of your account is also kept in BeanPool's key vault (see section 3 of our Privacy Policy). Most of deleting you can do yourself, at once, from inside the app.

1. Deleting data from a connected platform account (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, SoundCloud, RSS)

If you connected a publishing account so your public posts appear in your community's Pulse feed, there are two levels of removal.

Disconnect the channel

In the app: Settings → Channels & showcase, find the platform card, and tap Disconnect.

This immediately deletes the platform access token from your device and clears the channel's verified status on the node. No further posts are retrieved. Posts already listed in the feed remain visible until you delete them or the channel, or until newer posts from the same channel push them out under the retention limit described below.

Delete the channel

In the app: Settings → Channels & showcase, open the platform card, and tap Delete.

This is the complete removal. The node immediately scrubs the channel record and every feed item imported from it — the post URLs, titles, captions and thumbnail URLs are overwritten to NULL in the database, not merely hidden. What remains is each item's identifiers (the platform name and the platform's post identifier, and your member public key), its publication date and category, and timestamps, which exist only so that other nodes replicating the deletion converge on it.

The node also keeps a cached copy of each post's preview image so the feed loads quickly. Deleting the channel removes those cached images from the node's memory and disk in the same operation — not on their next request, and not when the cache next fills up. That is true whichever way an item goes: deleting a single post, deleting your account, the automatic retention pruning below, or a moderator taking an item down.

Automatic retention limit

Independently of anything you do, the node keeps only the 20 most recently published items from each channel. Older items beyond that are pruned automatically, with the same scrubbing applied. Items are not removed on age alone, so a channel that stops posting keeps its last 20 items until you delete them or the channel.

2. Deleting your entire BeanPool account

In the app: Settings → Account Deletion & Sign Out → Permanently Delete Account, and confirm by typing DELETE or your name.

This deletes your account at the community you are using, and takes your account off this phone for all your communities. Your accounts at other communities stay, so delete those first, or keep your 12 words to get back in and delete them afterwards. It does not delete your locked copy in the key vault: first tap Disconnect next to each sign-in account in Settings.

This erases the identity keypair from your device and, on the community node, scrubs your profile: your callsign, avatar, bio and contact details are cleared, your listings are cancelled, and your member record is reduced to an anonymous placeholder. Connected channels and every feed item imported from them are scrubbed in the same operation, so deleting your account also removes your syndicated Instagram or TikTok posts. Their cached preview images are removed from the node's memory and disk at the same moment, as described in section 1.

Any remaining balance is settled to your community's Commons Pool, since a mutual credit ledger has to stay in balance when a member leaves.

For the same reason, ledger entries recording trades between you and other members are retained in the community's transaction history. Those entries are what keep other members' balances correct, and erasing them would silently alter the record of trades other people made in good faith. They show "Deleted Member" instead of your name once your account is purged, but any note written on a trade stays as it was written.

Some other things stay too, such as messages you sent, your votes, the text and photos of your cancelled listings, and copies of the server's database made before you deleted. Section 13 of our Privacy Policy lists them all.

One condition: the node will refuse to purge an account with a trade still in escrow or a cross-node settlement in flight, because doing so would strand the other party. Complete or cancel those first, then purge.

3. Revoking BeanPool's access from the platform's own settings

Deleting data held by BeanPool is separate from revoking the authorisation you granted the platform. You can do that on the platform directly, and we recommend doing both:

  • Instagram: Settings and privacy → Website permissions → Apps and websites
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions

4. Requesting deletion by email

If you no longer have access to your device or your identity keypair, you cannot authenticate to the node, and the in-app routes above will not be available to you. In that case, email admin@beanpool.org with the community node you joined and the callsign or platform username you used.

We will action verified requests within 30 days. Please note that each community node is operated independently; where a node is run by a local operator rather than by us, we will pass your request to that operator and confirm to you when it has been actioned.
