## What they are

Your trust badge shows how much trust you have built:

- **Newcomer:** starting out;
- **Resident:** 200 trust;
- **Steward:** 600 trust;
- **Elder:** 1,400 trust.

A member who has not traded yet shows **FOUNDING** on their posts.

Trust comes from a vouch when you join, from completed trades, and from trust an admin has granted.

## They unlock nothing

Badges are recognition, not permission. Every member can post, trade, invite and vote, whatever their badge.

What your trust does change is how deep your credit line can go. See "Your credit line".

## Where to see them

- **Ledger**, then **Levels:** your badge, how much trust you have, and how much more to the next. **All levels** shows every badge; ones you have not reached yet are marked with a lock. That only means "not yet", not that something is locked away.
- **What builds your trust:** how much you have traded, with how many people, and your rating.
- On posts and profiles, next to each member's name.

In the **Market**, the **Trust** filter can show only posts from members with a certain badge or higher.

## Admins and badges

A community's admins can give a member a badge. That also gives them real credit, the same as earning it.
