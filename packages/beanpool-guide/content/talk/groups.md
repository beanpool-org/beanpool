## What a group is

A group is a place for some people rather than everyone: a working group, a project team, a guild or a social circle. Groups do not hold beans. Being in one does not change your trust or your vote.

Groups are in **Commons**, under **Groups**. Choose **My Groups** to see yours, or a kind of group to browse.

## Joining

Open a group, then:

- **Join Group** if it is open to anyone;
- **Request to Join** if a convenor approves members. You see **Request Pending Approval** until they do;
- groups marked **Invite Only** take new members by invitation. The screen for invitations is coming soon.

To leave, open the group and tap **Leave Group**.

## Posting to a group

Open the group and tap **Post to** and its name. Only the group's members see the post. It shows in the Market with a lock and the group's name. On the Market, the group buttons show only that group's posts.

## Starting a group

- Tap **+ Create a Group** in **Groups**.
- Give it a **Group Name** and a **Purpose / Description**.
- Choose a **Category**.
- Choose a **Join Policy**: **Open**, **Request to Join** or **Invite Only**.
- Tap **Create Group**. You become its **convenor**.

## Convenors

A convenor looks after the group. Under **Convenor Tools** they can:

- change the join policy;
- **Approve** or **Decline** requests to join;
- change a member's role to convenor, member or observer;
- remove a member.

A group always keeps at least one convenor.
