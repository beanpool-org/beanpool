## Add a channel

- In the **Pulse**, tap **+ Channels**. Or open **Settings** and tap **Channels & Showcase**.
- Tap **+ Add a channel**.
- Under **Where do you post?**, choose the site.
- Type or paste **Your link or handle**.
- Under **What's it about?**, choose a subject.
- Tap **Add channel**.

Each site works a little differently. The screen says which:

- **Updates itself** (YouTube, SoundCloud, a blog): new posts appear on the Pulse by themselves.
- **A tap per post** (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook): share each post by hand. Where the screen offers **Connect**, you can link the account instead.
- **Shows as a card** (a website).

## On each channel

- **Show on the local feed:** switch it off to keep this channel's posts off the Pulse.
- **+ Share post:** add one post by hand.
- **Make main:** choose your main video channel.
- **Remove:** take the channel off.

## Sharing one post

Tap **Share a post to Pulse**. Paste the **Post URL**, check which channel and subject it belongs to, and tap **Share to Pulse**.

## Remember

Whatever you share to the Pulse is shown with your name and photo. Anyone who opens your community's Pulse can see it, including people who have not joined.
