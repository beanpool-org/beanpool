## What it is

Your contacts list belongs to your account, not to a phone and not to one community. Sign in on any phone or browser, in any of your communities, and it is there. It is optional: nothing exists, on your phone or on any server, until you add a first contact.

It is not the same as Friends. A friend belongs to one community, and sees that you added them. Your contacts are private: nobody is told they are in your list, and it can hold people who are not on BeanPool. See "People and friends".

## Opening it

Tap **Chat**.

- **On the phone:** tap the people button beside the pencil, in the row with the search box. Until you use it or close it, a line under that row says **Bring your contacts with you.**, with **Import contacts** and ⓘ.
- **In the web app:** tap **👥 Import contacts** beside **✉️ New DM**. It reads **👥 Contacts** once you have someone in your list. The ⓘ beside it says how the list is kept.

The first row of **New Message**, in both apps, opens it too.

## Bringing people in

The **Contacts** screen has **＋ Add**, **Import** and **⋯**.

- **From this phone's contacts** (the phone app): tap **Import**, then **From this phone's contacts**. Only then does your phone ask whether BeanPool may see your contacts. BeanPool only reads them, never changes them, and reads them once: it doesn't watch them. To bring new ones later, tap **Import** again. If you say no, you can pick a contacts file or add people by hand.
- **WhatsApp** has no export of its own: it uses your phone's contacts, so importing them brings those too. Contacts you saved only inside WhatsApp aren't in your phone's contacts: save them to your phone in WhatsApp first.
- **From a contacts file (.vcf):** no permission is needed. A web page can't read your phone's contacts, so in the web app this is the way: export your contacts as a file, then bring the file in. **How do I export a contacts file?** on the phone, and the web app's Import page, show the steps for an Android phone, an iPhone and Google Contacts. The file is read on your phone or in your browser. It isn't uploaded.
- **Choose from this phone's contacts:** in a phone browser that offers it, such as Chrome on Android, the web app shows this button. Your browser shows your contacts and you tick who to bring.
- **By hand:** **＋ Add**, then a name, a phone number, an email and a note.

Before anything is added you see **Choose who to bring**, with everyone ticked except people already in your list and entries with no number or email. Untick anyone, then tap **Import**. Nothing is added, and nothing leaves your phone or browser, before you tap it.

You can keep up to 2,000 contacts. Each keeps a name, up to 5 numbers, up to 3 emails and a note. Photos, addresses and birthdays in a file are left out.

## People on BeanPool

Your list has two parts: **On BeanPool** and **Not on BeanPool yet**. BeanPool never matches phone numbers, so after an import everyone is under **Not on BeanPool yet**. A contact moves to **On BeanPool** only by your own hand:

- **In the web app,** on a member's profile, tap **＋ Add to my contacts**. The name you give is for you only, and they aren't told. If the name is already in your list, you are asked whether it is the same person. The profile then says **In your contacts as** and the name.
- **When someone joins with an invite you sent a contact** (below), and you say it was them.
- **On the phone,** **⋯**, then **Add people I've chatted with and my friends** adds everyone you have chatted with or added as a friend in the community you are on.

Under **On BeanPool**, **Message** opens a chat. If the person is in another of your communities, the button names it: the phone offers to switch you there, and the web app opens that community's web app. A member you blocked shows **Blocked** and **Unblock**. Blocking someone doesn't take them out of your contacts, and taking them out doesn't unblock them. Being in your contacts doesn't let anyone past message requests.

## Inviting someone

On someone under **Not on BeanPool yet**, tap **Invite**. It is one invite for one person, and it works once, for 30 days. Send it from your own apps: **Share…**, **Text message** (on the phone, when they have a number), **Email** (in the web app, when they have an email) or **Copy**. BeanPool sends nothing itself. The invite carries no note: their name, number and email are not sent to your community's server. The row then says when you invited them.

There is no button to invite everyone: each invite is one person, sent by you.

When someone joins with that invite, the row asks whether it was them. Links get passed on, so it asks rather than assumes. **Yes** moves them to **On BeanPool**. **No** leaves the contact as it was: someone else used the invite, and if that's a problem, tell an admin.

- In a community where only the admins invite people, the button asks an admin instead, with the usual ask. The admins will see the first name you type there.
- The global community takes no invites. There you can share its link instead. See "Joining BeanPool".
- Removing a contact whose invite is still out offers **Also cancel the invite I sent**.

## Where it is kept

- Your list is locked on your phone or in your browser with your account's key, the one your 12 words make. Only you can open it.
- Each of your communities keeps one locked copy for you. The people who run those servers can't read it, and nobody but you can make one in your name. A community you are only looking around as a guest gets none.
- The bottom of the **Contacts** screen says where your list is kept, such as **Kept on this phone and at:** and your communities' names. After a change, your list waits up to 15 minutes before it goes to your communities, so the time of a save doesn't say what you just did.
- The phone app keeps each of your communities up to date. The web app saves only to the community whose web app you have open.
- A community's owners can choose not to keep contact lists. Then the bottom of the screen says that community doesn't keep contact lists, and your list stays on your phone and at your other communities.
- On a new phone or in a new browser, once your account is there, your list comes back from your communities' copies: **Your contacts are back** and how many people.
- **Sign Out** in the web app deletes the list from that browser. If your latest changes haven't reached your community yet, Settings says so first and offers **Save now**. On the phone, the locked list stays under your account when you sign out, and nobody else on the phone can open it.
- **⋯**, then **Save a copy as a file (.vcf)**, gives you your whole list to take anywhere. That file is not locked: anyone who has it can read it. Keep it somewhere safe.

## What your community's server can and can't see

It can't see the names, numbers, emails or notes in your list, or which of them are BeanPool members. Nobody in your list is told.

It can see:

- that you keep a list there, and roughly how long it is: in steps of 16 KB, never a count of people;
- when it was saved, give or take that wait;
- what you do in the open, as it always could: that you made an invite (never for whom), who joined with it, and who you message.

It can refuse your copy, delete it, or hand back an older one. It can't read it, or make one in your name. An older copy can't undo a change your phone or browser already has, and a new phone given an older copy by one of your communities gets the newer one from another.

In the web app, the locking is done by the app your community's server sends to your browser, so a dishonest server could send an app that leaks your opened list. The phone app comes from the app store. The same is true of chats in the web app: see "Messages".

Chats between two people and group chats are end-to-end encrypted, always: there is nothing to turn on. Event chats and enterprise chats are not: your community's server can read those.

## Removing people

- Tap a contact, then **Remove from contacts**. Nobody is told. Your chats, and your friendship if you have one, stay as they are.
- **⋯**, then **Remove everyone**, empties your list on every device and in every community's copy: each community's copy becomes an empty locked list when your phone or browser next saves there. You can't undo it, so save a copy as a file first if you might want them.

## If you lose your phone

- Your 12 words, a linked sign-in, or your community's admins bringing your account back all give you the same key, so your list comes back with your account.
- If you lose your phone and your 12 words, and have no sign-in and no admin who can bring you back, your contacts list can't be opened again. Saving a copy as a file protects against that.
- If an admin then re-keys you, you get a new key, and your list was locked with the old one. The community that re-keyed you keeps that old locked list for a year. On the phone, **Contacts** says **Your contacts are locked with your old key. If you ever find your old 12 words, you can open them here.** **Open with my old 12 words** brings it into your new list, and **Delete it** deletes it for good. The web app says the same, and sends you to the phone app to open it. After a year it is deleted.
- A re-key moves your chats and friends to your new key, so on the phone **Add people I've chatted with and my friends** brings those people back. You can also import from your phone again.

If you delete your account in a community, or it removes you, that community deletes its copy. Your phone and your other communities keep theirs. See "Leaving or deleting your account".
