## Choosing your alerts

- Open **Settings**. Under **App Settings**, tap **Notification Preferences**.
- Switch each kind on or off:
- **Direct Messages:** when someone messages you.
- **Marketplace Activity:** requests, approvals and refusals on your deals, and what happened to a post you reported or one of yours the admins removed.
- **Held in Trust & System:** beans held, released or in question.

Changes save by themselves. They are kept by your community's server, so they follow your account.

Alerts about someone trying to recover your account always come through. You cannot turn those off.

## Event reminders

Under the switches, **Event reminders** sets how long before an event you are **Going** to or **Interested** in starts you are told. Tick **1 week**, **1 day**, **2 hours**, **1 hour** or **30 minutes** before — you can tick more than one — or tick **Off** for none at all.

Everyone starts on **1 day** before. This is your usual choice; any one event can be set differently on the event itself. See "Events".

Reminders arrive with your **Marketplace Activity** alerts, so they stop if you switch those off. You are never reminded about an event that was cancelled or has ended, or about a time that had already passed when you said you were coming. If the host moves the event, the reminder moves with it.

## No alerts at all?

Check that your phone allows notifications for BeanPool, in your phone's own settings.

## One chat at a time

You can mute one group's or enterprise's chat for 8 hours, a week or for good. See "Muting a chat".
