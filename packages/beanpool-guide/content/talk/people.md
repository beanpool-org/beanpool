## Finding people

Tap **Talk**, then **People**. There are three buttons:

- **Friends:** people you have added.
- **Community:** every member. Search by name, and sort by **Newest**, **Name**, **Friends**, **Trusted** or **Active**.
- **Invites:** invite someone new. See "Joining BeanPool". If you have not joined yet, this button says **Register**. When people have asked to join your community, it shows how many, and they are listed under **Wants to join**.

Tap a person to see their profile: their trust badge, trades, reviews and posts.

## Friends

- In **Community**, tap **+ Add** next to a name. It changes to **Added**. Tap **Added** to take them off again.
- In **Friends**, tap the message button to start a chat, or the remove button, then **Remove**, to take someone off.
- When someone adds you, the top of **Friends** says who, such as "Sam added you", with **Add back**. Tap their name to see their profile first. There is no notification. The line goes when you add them back, or after 30 days. Up to 20 are shown, then how many more.
- You can add up to 100 people in any 24 hours. Taking someone off counts too. If you reach it, the app says so and when you can add someone again.
- You can't add someone you have blocked: unblock them first. See "Blocking someone".

Friends are a shortcut for you. If you choose **Friends** for your contact details, only they can see them. See "Your profile".

## Connections in common

A member's profile can show **connections in common**: people you can ask about them. Someone is shown there only when you are friends both ways with someone who is friends both ways with them. That means you and that person have added each other, and that person and the member have added each other too. Adding someone on your own never puts anyone there, and nor does someone adding you. Up to 12 are shown. Tap a name to see their profile.

You can choose not to be shown as anyone's connection in common. In Settings, under **Legal & Privacy**, turn off **Show me as a connection in common**. Then nobody sees your name there, and you are not counted. It is on until you turn it off. See "Your privacy".

## Vouching for someone

Some members are chosen by the community to welcome newcomers. They can vouch for a new member, which gives that person a small credit line to start with. On the person's profile, they tap **Vouch for Member** and choose **Light** (25), **Standard** (50) or **Deep** (100). No Beans leave the voucher's own wallet.

Who may vouch is decided by an admin or by a community Decision, never by a badge. See "Rules and how decisions work".
