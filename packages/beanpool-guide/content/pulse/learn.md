## What is in it

In the **Pulse**, tap **Learn**. It holds:

- videos from BeanPool about how it works;
- the **Daily Reflection**, a short thought that changes every morning. It is just a heading; there is nothing to open.

Members can add their own how-to channels to Learn too, by choosing the subject **How it works** when they add a channel. Those videos are the members' own, not BeanPool's: check who posted a video before you follow its advice.

## Videos are extra

Everything you need is written in this guide. The videos are there if you would rather watch.

When BeanPool has made a video for a guide page, and it is in your community's Learn lane, the page shows **Watch:** and the video's name at the top. Tap it to open the video on YouTube. If there is no video for that page, or you are offline, the link simply does not show.

Only BeanPool's own videos ever appear on a guide page. A video a member adds to Learn never shows there, whatever it is called.

## Why a video might not show

- Your phone is offline, or cannot reach your community's server.
- A new BeanPool video only appears on guide pages after your community's server is updated.
- There is no video for that page yet.
