# Code of conduct

BeanPool is built for communities that look after each other. The same goes for the people building it and talking about it here, in Discussions, in issues and on r/beanpool.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith. People come from many countries, languages and levels of experience.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Help newcomers find their way.
- Keep other people's private information private: no names, addresses or messages that aren't yours.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or attacks on anyone for who they are or what they believe.
- Spam, scams, or advertising unrelated to BeanPool.
- Posting anyone's 12 words, keys, passwords or private messages.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block accounts that break these rules. Usually a reminder comes first; serious cases may be acted on straight away.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers by message to the moderators of [r/beanpool](https://www.reddit.com/message/compose?to=/r/beanpool), or use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment. For anything about security, use the [private security form](https://github.com/beanpool-org/beanpool/security/advisories/new).
