Last Updated: April 1, 2026

1. The Exchange Protocol Architecture

BeanPool functions strictly as decentralized mutual credit infrastructure. It is not a commercial financial institution, a regulated central exchange, or a custodial bank. By utilizing this software, you acknowledge that localized trades, mutual credit balances, and peer-to-peer exchanges are cooperative agreements between autonomous community members bound by local community compacts.

2. Community Balances & Non-Speculative Nature

Internal network tokens (represented as Beans) carry zero speculative fiat value. They represent mathematically signed network proofs recording reciprocal service exchange and trust on a zero-sum community ledger. Beans cannot be purchased with fiat debt, cannot be converted into speculative cryptocurrency pools, and are designed purely to facilitate direct community wealth exchange.

3. Zero Tolerance UGC & Safety Policy

To operate securely across public spaces, community networks enforce a strict, algorithmically and community-monitored zero-tolerance policy against abusive User-Generated Content (UGC):

  • No unlawful, threatening, or defamatory listings allowed.
  • No sexually explicit, illicit, or harmful imagery permitted.
  • No harassment, abusive communication, or discriminatory behavior tolerated.
  • Users can utilize the in-app shield/reporting tool on every post or profile to instantly block abusive users and flag them to community administrators.
  • Accounts verified as violating community safety compacts are removed from local node networks.

4. Open Source Disclaimer

Because BeanPool operates without centralized corporate intermediaries, it is provided "As-Is" under open-source licenses. The protocol developers make no warranties regarding continuous uptime or third-party device compatibility.
