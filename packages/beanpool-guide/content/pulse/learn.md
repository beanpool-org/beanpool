## What is in it

Open the **Pulse** and tap **How it works** along the top, on the phone and in the web app. It holds videos from BeanPool about how it works.

This is not **Learn**, your community's own pages of things worth knowing about where you live: see "Local knowledge".

Members can add their own how-to channels here too, by choosing the subject **How it works** when they add a channel. Those videos are the members' own, not BeanPool's: check who posted a video before you follow its advice.

## Videos are extra

Everything you need is written in this guide. The videos are there if you would rather watch.

On the phone, under **How it works**, tap a video's picture and it plays there in the card. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you tap. In the web app, tapping a video opens it on YouTube in a new tab. See "The Pulse".

When BeanPool has made a video for a guide page, and it is in your community's Pulse under **How it works**, the page shows **Watch:** and the video's name at the top. Tap it to open the video on YouTube. If there is no video for that page, or you are offline, the link simply does not show.

Only BeanPool's own videos ever appear on a guide page. A video a member adds under **How it works** never shows there, whatever it is called.

## Why a video might not show

- Your phone is offline, or cannot reach your community's server.
- A new BeanPool video only appears on guide pages after your community's server is updated.
- There is no video for that page yet.
