## What it is

Open the **Market** and tap **Pulse** near the top. The **Market** tab stays lit while the Pulse is open. In the web app it is also in the menu at the side, under **Market**.

It is on **Home** too, as the **The Pulse** card, when a neighbour has shared something in the last week: tap a post, or tap **See all**. If it is not on your Home, bring it back with **Add a card**. On the phone you can also open it from **Settings**, under **The Pulse**.

The Pulse gathers what members of your community post on other sites: videos, music, blogs, photos. It is a way to find out what your neighbours make and do.

- **Neighbours:** posts from members' channels. The buttons along the top pick a subject, like Food & growing or Repair & reuse. **All** shows everything.
- **Learn** (phone app): videos about how BeanPool works, and a short daily reflection. See "The Learn lane". In the web app they are in the feed, and **How it works** along the top shows only them.

Pull down to refresh. In the web app, tap 🔄 at the top instead.

On the phone, the buttons along the top slide away as you scroll down. Scroll up a little and they come back.

## Opening a post

**A YouTube video:** on the phone, tap the picture, with the ▶ on it, and the video plays here in the card. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you tap — scrolling past a video sends nothing to anyone. Tap the button in the corner of the video to fill the screen.

The video stops when you scroll it out of sight, when you leave the Pulse, and when you start a different one. Only one plays at a time.

In the web app a video does not play in the card. Tap the picture or the title and it opens on YouTube in a new tab.

**Everything else** — TikTok, Instagram, a blog, a website: tap the picture or the title and it opens in your browser or in that site's own app.

You can always open a video on YouTube instead: tap the title, or **Open on YouTube ↗** underneath it. If a video will not play here — some people do not allow their videos to play outside YouTube, and some phones cannot — the card says so and offers you that button.

## Something wrong with a post

- **Someone else's post:** tap the flag, choose why (**Spam or scam**, **Offensive content**, **Misleading post**, **Child safety** or **Other**) and tap **Submit report** (**Submit Report** in the web app). The people who run your community's server look at it.
- **Your own post:** tap **Hide**, then **Hide from feed**. It comes off the Pulse for everyone. A hidden post can't be brought back. In the web app you can also tap **Delete**, then **Delete permanently**, to remove your post for good.

You cannot hide someone else's post just for yourself.

## Adding your own

Tap **Add to Pulse** at the bottom right. When the Pulse is empty, the button is in the middle of the page instead. To share a post, add the channel it is on first: tap **Your channels** at the top. See "Sharing your channels".

On the global community the Pulse asks nothing of you yet: there is no **Add to Pulse**, and the web app does not suggest posts to share. That waits until adding there has rules for new members. **Your channels** works there as on any community: add a channel and its posts still come in, and you can share a post from there.
