# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Start with the [members' guide](https://beanpool.org/guide/). Still stuck? Ask in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/beanpool-org/beanpool/discussions/categories/q-a) or on [r/beanpool](https://www.reddit.com/r/beanpool/).
- **Something isn't working:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/beanpool-org/beanpool/issues/new/choose).
- **An idea:** [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/beanpool-org/beanpool/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **A security problem:** report it [privately](https://github.com/beanpool-org/beanpool/security/advisories/new). Never in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Running your own community server:** the operator manual is in the manager under Help, and in [`packages/beanpool-guide/operators`](packages/beanpool-guide/operators).

Your community's own organisers can help with anything about your community: its members, its rules, its Beans.

Never share your 12 words, passwords or recovery codes with anyone, including us. Nobody from BeanPool will ever ask for them.
