## What a group is

A group is a place for some people rather than everyone: your street, a garden crew, a choir, a guild. Every group has its own chat. Groups do not hold beans. Being in one does not change your trust or your vote.

## Your groups

Tap **Talk**, then **Groups**. Every group you are in is listed there, newest message first, with the enterprises you keep (🥖) and the events you are going to (📅). Each shows its latest message and how many you have not read.

- The number on **Groups** at the top adds up your unread messages, except in chats you have muted.
- Tap a group to open its chat. That marks it read.
- Pull the list down to refresh it.

The same list is in **Commons**, under **Groups**, as **Your groups**, without the unread numbers. It opens the same chats.

## Starting a group

- In **Talk**, under **Groups**, tap **New group**. Or in **Commons**, under **Groups**, tap the round **+** button. Both open the same **Create a Group** form.
- Give it a **Group Name** and, if you like, a **Purpose / Description**.
- Choose a **Category**: **Social Circle** is chosen for you; the others are **General**, **Working Group**, **Project Team** and **Guild**.
- Choose a **Join Policy**: **Open**, **Request to Join** or **Invite Only**.
- Tap **Create Group**. You become its **convenor**, and the app takes you straight into the new group's chat.

Running something together, with beans involved? Tap **Start an enterprise** at the bottom of the categories instead. See "Enterprises".

## Inviting people

A new group's chat asks **Who do you want to invite?**

- Tap **Invite people**, tick the people you want, and tap the button at the bottom (for example **Invite 2 people**).
- Or tap **Not now** to skip it.

Later, a convenor can invite people from the chat's **⋮** menu, **Invite people**. Each person you invite sees the group in **Commons**, under **Groups**, marked **INVITED**. When they join, the chat says so.

## The group's chat

- Tap the group's name at the top of the chat to see the group: its members, **Leave Group**, and for convenors, **Convenor Tools**.
- The **⋮** menu has **Invite people** (convenors), **Mute notifications** and **Group info**.
- Joins, leaves and role changes show in the chat as small grey lines.
- Write **@** and someone's name to alert them. See "Mentions".

A group's chat is not end-to-end encrypted. Its members and the people who run your community's server can read it. The chat says so above the message box. Private things belong in a chat between two people.

## Joining a group

In **Commons**, under **Groups**, **Groups you could join** lists the groups you are not in. Filter them by kind with the buttons above the list. Open a group, then:

- **Join Group** if it is open to anyone;
- **Request to Join** if a convenor approves members. You see **Request Pending Approval** until they do.

An **Invite Only** group is hidden from everyone who is not in it or invited to it.

If a convenor removed you from a group, you cannot join it again by yourself.

## Joining by invitation

A group you are invited to shows in **Commons**, under **Groups**, marked **INVITED**. Tap it to see the invitation: who invited you, what the group is, who is in it, and that its chat is not private.

- Tap **Join the group**. You land in its chat.
- Tap **Not now** to decide later. The invitation stays.
- If it says **This invitation is no longer open**, it was withdrawn. Ask whoever invited you to invite you again.
- If it says **Couldn't open this group**, your phone could not reach your community's server. Tap **Try again**.

## Posting to a group

Tap the group's name at the top of its chat, then **Post to** and its name. Only the group's members see the post. It shows in the Market with a lock and the group's name.

## Convenors

A convenor looks after the group. Under **Convenor Tools** they can:

- change the join policy;
- **Approve** or **Decline** requests to join;
- change a member's role to convenor, member or observer;
- remove a member.

The last convenor cannot step down or leave while anyone else is still in the group.
