3. Your account

  • Your account is a key made on your device, and your 12 words rebuild it. We cannot reset them. Keep them private: anyone who has them has your account. You are responsible for what is done with your key.
  • If you link a Google, Apple or Facebook sign-in, or join the global community with one, a locked copy of your account is kept in our key vault (section 8). [MARTY: true only if the release build carries the live vault; a build without it keeps the copy at your community]

  • If you lose your key and have no words and no sign-in, an admin of your community may re-key your account to a new phone with a one-time code; the admin confirms your phone in person. An admin may refuse.

4. Beans

  • Beans are mutual credit inside one community: a record, on that community's server, of value given and received. Everyone starts at zero. A positive balance means the community owes you; a negative one means you owe it.
  • Beans are not money, not a currency, not a security and not a crypto-asset. They have no cash value, cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for money through BeanPool, and are not redeemable from BeanPool or from any operator. [LAWYER: Australian financial-services and non-cash-payment-facility law; GST and income tax on barter credits for members who trade in business (ATO barter rulings); whether a statement to members is required.]
  • Credit comes from a vouch, an admin's grant and completed trades, down to −2,000 Beans; how much of it you may use depends on how many offers you keep active. If you drop below the offers your debt needs, spending on credit pauses. These numbers are the software's defaults; a community's owner can set a different floor for confirmed members, and an admin can lower or freeze one member's.
  • Fees: 1.5 % of a marketplace sale or of a pay-out a Decision makes, and a monthly circulation fee on the part of a positive balance above 200, both paid into that community's Commons pool. Gifts between members are free. The community decides what the Commons is spent on. Nothing goes to BeanPool.
  • When you leave with a balance, it is settled with the Commons. A debt left behind in a known community stays on its record for three years and may be paid back, worked off or forgiven by a Decision.
  • A trade is between its two members. BeanPool and the operator guarantee neither that the other side performs nor the quality of anything offered. Escrow holds Beans, not goods. Disputes are settled by the community's admins under its rules, and those looks at your trade are logged.
  • Beans on the global community: none. Global is for finding people and communities.

5. What you may and may not do

  • Follow the rules of each community you join, and its operator's decisions about its own space.
  • Post only what you have the right to post. You keep ownership of what you write and photograph; you give the community's server, its standby and its members the right to store and show it as the app does, including after you leave, in the forms the Privacy Policy describes.
  • Not allowed anywhere: anything unlawful; content that sexualises or endangers children (zero tolerance, reported to the authorities; see Child Safety); threats, harassment, slurs; scams and fraud, including trades that only move Beans around; posting anyone's 12 words, keys or private messages; impersonation; scraping, probing or attacking servers (report a weakness privately through GitHub's form instead); reselling or re-publishing members' data.
  • The Pulse. If you connect TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, SoundCloud, a website or a feed, you confirm you own or may syndicate that content, and you follow that platform's terms. Your platform tokens are kept on your device.
  • Moderation. Any member can report. A community's moderators review reports and may remove posts; its owner and admins may also suspend or remove members, and a community may vote on removing a member. On global, posts reported by enough independent members are hidden until a moderator looks, repeat offenders are muted, new accounts have daily limits, and accounts that joined together from one connection can be acted on together. Decisions on a local community are its own; on global, the BeanPool team decides, and there is no appeal beyond admin@beanpool.org [MARTY].

6. Running a community

If you run a BeanPool server you are its operator. You accept the MIT licence for the software, and:

  • You are responsible to your members for their data, under your own law: you hold their profiles, trades, chats that are not end-to-end encrypted, and backups. Publish your own privacy information if your law requires it. [LAWYER]
  • You moderate your community and answer its reports, including child-safety reports first.
  • A beanpool.org name is ours, lent to your server's key: up to three names per key; a name is paused when a different server answers behind it, and we may pause, block or release a name, for example for abuse or impersonation; some names are reserved and some need our approval. Traffic to that name passes through Cloudflare in our account, which can read it; a name on your own domain does not. We may change or end the address service with notice [MARTY: how much].
  • The directory lists your community unless you switch it off; what it shows is your choice in Settings.
  • Off-box backups go to a store you choose, locked to your recovery code and your owners; we never hold them.
  • Linking to other communities is off unless you turn it on, and cross-community trades are between the two operators' servers.

7. The global community

We run global.beanpool.org as a public lobby. Today it is a private preview; at the release it opens to anyone with 12 words or a sign-in. There are no Beans and no community votes. Visitors who have not joined see members' listings with no names and only a rough area; members see where each listing is placed. We may remove posts or accounts, reset or close the community, or change what it does, and we will say so on this website.

8. The key vault

If you link a sign-in, we keep a locked copy of your account in our key vault in Iceland. We promise: the vault checks your sign-in itself; every restore waits 24 hours unless one of your own devices approves it; your devices are warned and can stop it; Disconnect deletes your copy at once, and the vault's own locked backups drop it within 30 days; we open a copy only to give it back to you or under a valid court order, and we tell you unless forbidden. We do not promise that the vault's host, a compromised sign-in account, or a court cannot reach a copy; the Privacy Policy says what we cannot protect you from. Your 12 words always work without the vault. [LAWYER: a consumer service with promises; what the Australian Consumer Law implies and what can be limited.]
