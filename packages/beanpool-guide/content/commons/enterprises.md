## What they are

An **enterprise** is something people run together for the community: a tool library, a market stall, a community garden. It has its own beans, its own Offers and Needs, and its own credit line. It is run by **keepers**.

- A **project** has a goal and usually a deadline, like raising 500 beans for a new roof.
- An **ongoing** enterprise keeps going.

## Finding them

Tap **Commons**, then **Enterprises**. The **Community Treasuries** come first, then every enterprise with its status, purpose and balance. Tap one to open it.

## What an enterprise page shows

Every member can see:

- its **Purpose**;
- its **Balance**, credit line, live Offers and surplus;
- **Income & Spend**: every bean in and out;
- its keepers;
- its **Listings**, **Recent activity** and a **Discussion** you can write in.

At the bottom, **Report Enterprise** if something is wrong.

## Backing an enterprise

- **Pledge beans to a project:** on its page, under the funding box, enter an amount and tap **Pledge Beans**.
- **Trade with it:** its Offers and Needs are in the Market like anyone's.
- **Become a keeper:** see "Keepers".

## Starting one

- Tap **Commons**, then **Enterprises**, then the round **+** button.
- Choose **Bounded Project** or **Ongoing**.
- Give it a **Name** and a **Purpose statement**.
- For a project, set a **Funding goal** and, if you like, a deadline.
- Add a photo if you like.
- Tap **Start Initiative**.

It starts straight away, and you are its lead keeper. There is no community vote to start one.

The name, purpose and photo cannot be changed in the app yet.
