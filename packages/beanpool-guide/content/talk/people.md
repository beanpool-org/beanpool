## Finding people

Tap **Chat**, then **People**. In the web app, tap **People** in the row of tabs. There are three buttons:

- **Friends:** people you have added.
- **Community:** every member. Search by name. On the phone, sort by **Newest**, **Name**, **Friends**, **Trusted** or **Active**. On the global community it shows the members nearest your area: type at least 2 letters of a name to find anyone else.
- **Invites:** invite someone new. See "Joining BeanPool". If you have not joined yet, this button says **Register**. When people have asked to join your community, it shows how many, and they are listed under **Wants to join**. In the web app this button is always **🎟️ Invites**, never **Register**. It shows how many have asked too, such as **🎟️ Invites (2)**, with the same **Wants to join** list.

Tap a person to see their profile: their reviews and posts, and, on the phone in a community with Beans, their trust badge and trades. You can message them straight from their profile with **Message**, or add them as a friend with **+ Add friend**. See "Your profile".

## Friends

- In **Community**, tap 💬 to start a chat, or tap **+ Add** next to a name. It changes to **Added**. Tap **Added** to take them off again. In the web app it changes to **✓ Friend**, which you can't tap: to take someone off, use **Remove** under **Friends**. You can also message someone or add them as a friend straight from their profile.
- In **Friends**, tap 💬 to start a chat, or the remove button, then **Remove**, to take someone off.
- When someone adds you, the top of **Friends** says who, such as "Sam added you", with **Add back**. Tap their name to see their profile first. There is no notification. The line goes when you add them back, or after 30 days. Up to 20 are shown, then how many more.
- You can add up to 100 people in any 24 hours. Taking someone off counts too. If you reach it, the app says so and when you can add someone again.
- You can't add someone you have blocked: unblock them first. See "Blocking someone".

Friends are a shortcut for you. If you choose **Friends** for your contact details, only they can see them. See "Your profile".

Nobody else can read your friends list. The person you add sees that you did.

## Connections in common

A member's profile can show **connections in common**: people you can ask about them. Someone is shown there only when you are friends both ways with someone who is friends both ways with them. That means you and that person have added each other, and that person and the member have added each other too. Adding someone on your own never puts anyone there, and nor does someone adding you. Up to 12 are shown. Tap a name to see their profile.

You can choose not to be shown as anyone's connection in common. In Settings, under **Legal & Privacy**, turn off **Show me as a connection in common**. Then nobody sees your name there, and you are not counted. It is on until you turn it off. See "Your privacy".

## Showing credit

This is only for people who give credit: the owners, admins and vouchers. Any other member, and a moderator, sees no switch and their app never asks for the data.

In **Community**, above the search, **Show credit** is a switch. It is off every time you open People, so opening People to message a friend is not a look. Under it once it is on: "Only people who can give credit see this. Each look is recorded."

With it on, each row says in plain words what the person has:

- Their credit line and how much of it they can use now: **Credit line 1,300 · can use all of it now**, or **can use 400 of it now**, or **asleep until they post an Offer**. For someone with none: **No credit yet · 4 live Offers**. A line frozen by an admin reads **Credit line frozen by an admin** to owners and admins, and **Can’t be given credit right now** to a voucher.
- Their balance, with its sign, beside their line: **Balance −420 · using 420 of 1,300**, **Balance +340** or **Balance 0**. If they owe more than their line allows it says how much more.
- Their **credit risk**, one word with its shape: ○ not known yet, ✓ low, ! medium, ✕ high. The reasons are under it on the **Give credit** sheet and on the profile. See "Credit risk: what it reads".
- One button, **Give credit**.

Tap anywhere else on the row to open the profile. The chips **All**, **No credit yet**, **Has Offers** and **My people** narrow the list. **Has Offers** is people with no credit yet who have a live Offer, because credit you give them can be used at once. **My people** is the people you invited or vouched for. People with no credit yet come first, and of them those with a live Offer first. A voucher also sees their own budget line above the chips, such as "Vouching budget: 150 of 1,000 left", with **Ask for more** beside it. Owners and the credit committee have one more chip, **Vouchers**, with each voucher's budget left, a flag when it is running low and a one-tap top-up.

The list comes from your community's server each time and is not kept on your phone, so offline it says "Connect to your community to see credit lines." Enterprises, visitors and removed accounts are never listed. A paused member is listed greyed, with no button, and your own row has none. Every look is recorded: who looked, at whom, on which day.

## Giving credit

**Give credit** opens a sheet over the list, so the list keeps its place. It shows the person's line and balance and their credit risk with the reasons, then what you can do:

- **Vouch for them:** pick **25**, **50**, **75** or **100** Beans (an owner also **200** or **500**), from your budget, as below.
- **Confirm them as known** (owners and admins): this adds the known floor. On the phone it opens the names list's form with their BeanPool name filled in, so the names list's own checks apply. The web app says it is done in the BeanPool app on your phone.
- **Raise their known floor** (owners and admins): for a member who is already confirmed, never your own, in steps of 100 and no higher than the cap. It only raises: lowering and freezing stay in Settings, so a tap here can never cut someone's credit.

If the person is below zero, the presets are greyed under the sentence "@sam is below zero: a credit-line gift or a Decision can help.". If an admin froze their credit line, they are greyed under "@sam can’t be given part of a credit line right now." After a give the sheet says what happened, such as "Vouched 500 for @sam. Their credit line is now 500.", and **Next ›** opens the next person in the filter. Credit you give can't be taken back, so each one is a look at one person. The same card sits on a profile, above the Vouch box, for the same people.

## Vouching for someone

A voucher can vouch for a member who has already joined, giving them a credit line to start trading with. Vouchers are owners by role, admins an owner has made vouchers, and members set as vouchers on the names list by an owner or credit-committee admin.

On the member's profile, a voucher taps **Vouch for @name** and chooses **25**, **50**, **75** or **100** Beans, from their voucher budget. An owner can also choose **200** or **500**, and has no budget limit ("From your vouching budget: no limit"). No Beans leave the voucher's own wallet.

- **One vouch per person:** each voucher can vouch for a person once. You can later raise your vouch for them, up to 100 Beans in total (500 for an owner).
- **At most 3 vouchers:** at most 3 vouchers can vouch for the same person (up to 300 Beans total).
- **Up to 500 with an owner:** once an owner has vouched for someone, they can hold up to 500 Beans of vouches in all. An owner can top up someone who already has 3 vouchers, to 500 at most.
- **Never below zero:** you cannot vouch for a member who is below zero now. To help someone in debt, members can give part of their credit line on Send, or propose a community Decision.
- **Not while it is frozen:** you cannot vouch for a member whose credit line an admin froze. The app says so.
- **Running low and asking for more:** a voucher with under 100 Beans left can no longer give anyone a full vouch, and with under 25 the app says "Nothing left to vouch with". **Ask for more** is one tap, with no text and no amount, and it goes to the owners and the credit committee. You can ask once a week. When an owner or committee admin raises your budget, the ask is cleared.

See "Joining BeanPool" and "Your credit line".
