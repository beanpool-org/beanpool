## Offer first

You must have posted at least one **Offer** before you can post a **Need** or take up someone's Offer. Everyone brings something. How many Offers you have up also decides how far below zero you can go. See "Your credit line".

## Before you start

You need a name and a profile photo. If one is missing, the app asks you to **Set up profile** first.

## Posting

- Tap **+ ADD POST** on the Market tab or the Map.
- Choose **Offer** or **Need**. The form opens on the Map.
- **Title:** a few words, up to 50 characters. "Sourdough loaf", "Lift to town on Fridays".
- **Category:** pick the closest one.
- **Description:** what, when, where, anything people should know.
- **Photos:** at least one, up to five. Tap **Camera** or **Gallery**.
- **Location:** tap **My location**, or **Drop a pin** and tap the map, then **Confirm Pin**. The pin is shown on the map to everyone in your community, so drop it near you rather than on your front door if you prefer.
- **Price:** a number of beans. Tap the unit button to choose **Total**, **/ Hr**, **/ Dy**, **/ Wk** or **/ Mo**. Tap **FREE** to give it away.
- Tap **Post Offer** or **Post Need**.

Until everything needed is filled in, the button tells you what is missing, like **Add a photo** or **Set a price**.

## Not sure what to charge?

Tap **Not sure what to charge?** under the price. A rough guide: 40 beans is about an hour of someone's time. The **Community Pricing Guide** has estimates for more than 500 things. Tapping one fills in an Offer for you.

Below the price you see what you will receive after the 1.5% market fee. Gifts have no fee.

## Extra choices

- **Repeatable:** the post stays up after each deal, and several people can take it at once. Good for a weekly lift or bread every Saturday.
- **Cash also needed:** tick this if you also need some cash, for fuel or materials. The app never handles cash; you sort that out between you.
- **Where does this travel?** Only if your community trades with other communities: keep the post here, or share it further.

The app keeps a draft as you type. If you leave and come back, it asks whether to **Continue** or **Start fresh**.

## Posting only to a group

Open **Commons**, then **Groups**, then your group, and tap **Post to** and the group's name. Only that group sees it. This shorter form has no photos and no location.

## Changing or removing a post

Open your post. If no deal is under way on it:

- **Edit Post** changes the words, category, price and photos. Tap **Save**. The location cannot be changed; delete the post and post it again instead.
- **Pause Offer** hides an Offer for a while. **Activate Offer** brings it back. Needs cannot be paused.
- **Delete Post** removes it. Anyone still asking for it is told no.

Posts do not expire. Delete them when they are done.
