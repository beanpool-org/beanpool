## Finding people

Tap **Talk**, then **People**. There are three buttons:

- **Friends:** people you have added.
- **Community:** every member. Search by name, and sort by **Newest**, **Name**, **Friends**, **Trusted** or **Active**.
- **Invites:** invite someone new. See "Joining BeanPool". If you have not joined yet, this button says **Register**. When people have asked to join your community, it shows how many, and they are listed under **Wants to join**.

Tap a person to see their profile: their trust badge, trades, reviews and posts.

## Friends

- In **Community**, tap **+ Add** next to a name. It changes to **Added**. Tap **Added** to take them off again.
- In **Friends**, tap the message button to start a chat, or the remove button, then **Remove**, to take someone off.

Friends are a shortcut for you. If you choose **Friends** for your contact details, only they can see them. See "Your profile".

## Vouching for someone

Some members are chosen by the community to welcome newcomers. They can vouch for a new member, which gives that person a small credit line to start with. On the person's profile, they tap **Vouch for Member** and choose **Light** (25), **Standard** (50) or **Deep** (100). No beans leave the voucher's own wallet.

Who may vouch is decided by an admin or by a community Decision, never by a badge. See "Rules and how decisions work".
