BeanPool is made by a small project, not by you. "Suggest a change" is how members, and you, tell the project about an idea or a problem.

![The Settings Home screen where operator shortcuts and feedback live](images/home.webp)

## Where it goes

A suggestion goes straight from the member's app or browser to the BeanPool project at beanpool.org. It does not pass through your server, and you and your members do not see it.

## Is it on yet?

Not in this version. Every "Suggest a change" button, in the apps and in Settings, stays hidden until the project switches the service on. Until then, raise problems on the BeanPool project's GitHub page.

## What is kept

- The text (10 to 2000 characters), whether it is an idea, a problem or other, where it came from (app, web app or Settings), the app version, the device type and the language.
- The community's name, only if the person types it.
- Not kept: the person's name, key or internet address.

There are limits on how many suggestions one connection can send in an hour and in a day.

## Who reads it

The BeanPool project team reads the suggestions and sorts them each week. They choose which ones to share publicly on the project's GitHub. Nobody replies through the app, so if you want an answer, say how to reach you in the text.
