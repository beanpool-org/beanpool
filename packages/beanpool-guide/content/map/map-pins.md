## Pins

Tap **Map** at the bottom of the screen. The map shows your community's posts:

- **Green pins:** Offers.
- **Orange pins:** Needs.
- **Purple pins with a calendar:** events that have not happened yet.

A pin shows the post's category picture. Where many pins are close together, they join into a blue circle with a number. Zoom in to see them one by one.

People, groups and enterprises are not on the map. Neither are polls.

## Tapping a pin

- **An Offer or a Need:** a card shows the title and who posted it. Tap **View Details** to open it, or their name to see their profile. Tap the map or **✕** to close the card.
- **An event:** a card shows when and where, and how many are going. Tap **View event**.

## Filters

At the top of the map:

- **All**, **Offers**, **Needs** or **Events**. Tap **✕** to clear.
- **All Categories** to show one category.
- For events: **Today**, **This weekend** or **Next 7 days**. The map moves to fit them.

## Buttons on the left

- The moon or sun: a dark or light map.
- The target: show where you are. The first time, the app asks for permission to use your location.
- **+** and **−**: zoom.

The amber circle shows the area your community serves.

## Adding a post from the map

Tap **+ ADD POST**. See "Posting an Offer or a Need".
